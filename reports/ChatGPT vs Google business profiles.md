# Google Business Profile beats ChatGPT's new business profile

**For a small Indian business today, Google Business Profile (GBP) wins clearly; the ChatGPT profile is a free side task, not a replacement.** GBP is free in India ([Google India](https://business.google.com/in/business-profile/)), it is built into Search and Maps, and Google's own AI answers draw on it. OpenAI's "Businesses in ChatGPT" beta (Business Hub) only launched on 10 Nov 2026, four days before this research date. It is also free for now ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)). But OpenAI says local stores and restaurants can claim only an existing US listing, and Business Agents are limited to US ecommerce and online-service businesses ([OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)). India is neither confirmed nor excluded for website-based profiles. AI chatbots send only about **0.3% to 1.1% of website traffic**, while organic search sends about **43%** ([SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/blog/ai-traffic-study/); [Conductor via RWE](https://remoteworkeurope.eu/news/2026/conductor-aeo-geo-benchmarks-ai-referrals-one-percent)). For a freelancer, the best offer is GBP setup plus "clean data everywhere", with the ChatGPT claim as a free add-on for clients who have a website. Selling "ChatGPT listing setup" alone cannot be backed by any outcome data yet. There is **no outcome data at all** for ChatGPT profiles, and most numbers below come from vendors or search summaries, so treat them as indicative.

## How to read this report: verified, reported, assumed

Three labels are used. **Verified** means the company's own page text was seen (through search extracts, because direct page fetches were blocked in this session, so some official text may be truncated). **Reported** means press, agency or vendor sources whose figures were not independently checked. **Assumed** means my own reasoning, and each such item is marked "my judgment" or "my arithmetic". Almost every statistic about audience, traffic, prices and AI accuracy is *reported*, and much of it is from companies that sell SEO or AI-visibility services. The scorecard and the probabilities are opinion, not measurement.

The two are different kinds of thing. GBP is a mature listing that people see on Maps and in the local pack. The ChatGPT profile is a data input to an assistant that, by OpenAI's own words, decides independently what to recommend. The business model is also different. OpenAI's free agent is a funnel toward paid Sponsored Business Agents inside ChatGPT Ads ([OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001651-setting-up-a-sponsored-business-agent-in-ads-manager)). ChatGPT Ads are self-serve in India with daily budgets from about ₹725, reported by Indian press ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/openai-chatgpt-ads-users-india-free-go-plans-chatbot-advertising-free-plans-126082700310_1.html)). Whether sponsored agents work for Indian accounts is not stated.

## ChatGPT is huge in India, but the traffic it sends is tiny

ChatGPT's audience is large. OpenAI cites **1.2 billion weekly users** in its launch post ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)). OpenAI's India head said in early November that India's weekly users have doubled since January and that India is now its largest market ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/india-becomes-chatgpts-largest-market-globally-user-base-doubles-since-january/)). In February Sam Altman had put India at over 100 million weekly users ([TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-india-100-million-weekly-chatgpt-users/)). That suggests roughly **200 million**, which is my arithmetic, not a company figure. Google's reach is of the same order: the Gemini app and AI Mode each report over 1 billion monthly users ([Alphabet Q3 2026](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/alphabet-earnings-q3-2026/); [Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/alphabet-q2-earnings-call-highlights)). Search handles more than 5 trillion queries a year, an old January 2025 figure ([DemandSage](https://demandsage.com/google-search-statistics/)). Weekly and monthly users are different units, so they cannot be compared directly. No clean official count exists for Google Maps users in India.

What matters to a shop is customers, not users. Studies of website traffic put AI referrals at **0.32% of traffic** ([SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/blog/ai-traffic-study/)), **0.42%** of sessions in August ([SEOcrawl](https://seocrawl.ai/blog/ai-traffic-vs-organic-search-study)) and **1.08%** ([Conductor via RWE](https://remoteworkeurope.eu/news/2026/conductor-aeo-geo-benchmarks-ai-referrals-one-percent)). In the same SE Ranking data organic search was 42.75%, so Google sent about 134 times more visitors than AI platforms. ChatGPT accounts for roughly three-quarters or more of AI referrals. Claims that AI visitors convert 4x to 23x better come from vendors and single sites, and another study found ChatGPT traffic converting 13% worse ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/why-every-ai-search-study-tells-a-different-story-465511)). None of this isolates local shops or India. Local shops are mostly reached by calls, directions and walk-ins, which web referral counts miss, so the true AI influence is probably understated. That is my judgment.

The trend favours "layering, not replacing". Google reported its fastest query growth in five years ([Digit](https://www.digit.in/news/general/googles-investments-in-ai-are-driving-more-searches-sundar-pichai-reveals-during-q3-2026-earnings-call.html)). The one local-specific warning sign is a US survey of 1,227 consumers: use of AI tools for local recommendations rose from **6% to 45%** in a year, while Google's share of review-platform use fell from 83% to 71% ([BrightLocal](https://brightlocal.com/reports/local-seo-statistics/)). That 45% means "has ever used", not "mostly uses", and OpenAI's own usage study found only about 2.1% of ChatGPT conversations concern buying products ([Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/articles/openai-just-shared-people-really-073757816.html)).

## Google feeds Google's AI, ChatGPT mostly looks elsewhere

A strong Google profile helps with Google's AI surfaces directly and with ChatGPT only indirectly. In a BrightLocal vendor study of 200,085 prompts in the US, UK and Australia, GBP supplied **28.5% of all citations**, and the study's own conclusion was that GBP "is hardly used by ChatGPT" ([BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-ai-visibility-study/)). A companion study found Yelp was a source in 80% of ChatGPT answers ([BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/resources/ai-directory-sources/)). Business websites made up 42% of citations. Google Maps mentioned a tracked business in 66% of searches against 32% to 38% for the AI surfaces, and a repeated ChatGPT prompt returned an overlapping result set only 23% of the time. That volatility means a one-time listing cannot promise stable visibility. A second vendor study of mostly US chains found ChatGPT recommended **1.2% of locations** against 35.9% for the Google 3-pack ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/ai-local-visibility-report-2026-468085)). All of this is US/UK/Australia data. No study covers how ChatGPT picks Indian local businesses, and Yelp is thin in India. My judgment is that Justdial and similar directories matter more there.

The things that help every platform are the same: a real website, consistent name, address, phone and hours, and good review ratings. Accuracy is a shared risk. In a 10,000-business US test, ChatGPT's details fully matched Google's in **55.6%** of cases, with wrong phone numbers in 30.1% (the authors note call-tracking numbers inflate this) ([Insites](https://insites.com/blog/does-chatgpt-hallucinate-local-businesses-the-data-on-ai-accuracy/)). Google itself warns its AI answers "can sometimes be incorrect".

Google's profile is not trouble-free. Calls fell 11.9% and website clicks 12.5% year on year in Q2 2026 in the US while direction requests rose 21.1% ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/calls-clicks-falling-google-maps-destination-486276)). Owners report opaque suspensions and lost reviews after reinstatement: in a small non-random sample of 198 help-forum threads, 63% had already failed an appeal ([Azizi Technologies](https://azizitechnologies.co.uk/google-business-profile-suspension-study)). Review extortion is documented, and Google has a Merchant Extortion form ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/16404809?hl=en)). These are real risks, but they are the risks of a channel that sends customers. The ChatGPT profile has no comparable proof it sends any.

## Who can benefit in India: most shops have no website

India has about **10.1 crore** Udyam and Udyam Assist registrations ([MSME Dashboard](https://dashboard.msme.gov.in/)), but this counts registrations, not operating shops, and most are informal micro units. Reliable data on GBP use is missing. Small surveys put website ownership at roughly 20% to 27% ([India SME Forum](https://indiasmeforum.org/digishaastra/assets/docs/Final-META-Report-Card-2025.pdf)), and a vendor sample of 66,452 map-listed independent businesses found **18.4%** had their own website ([Raveneye](https://raveneyeglobal.com/research/small-business-ai-visibility-india-2026-07/)). OpenAI's claim route is "initially available to eligible businesses with a website". So most Indian kirana-scale shops are shut out of the ChatGPT profile today, while GBP needs only a phone and a shopfront. Indian shops also lean on WhatsApp and Instagram. Google has also added Hindi and seven other Indian languages to AI Mode ([Google India](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/google-search-ai-mode-now-available-in-hindi/)), and OpenAI says it is prioritising Hindi ([Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/info-tech/chatgpt-usage-doubles-in-india-over-past-year-openai-expands-hindi-voice-capabilities/article71589092.ece)). Accuracy for Indian local businesses in Hindi is untested in any study I found.

Justdial complicates the picture in smaller cities. Reported figures put it at 51 million+ listings with paid plans of ₹30,000 to ₹1,00,000+ a year ([Namo Marketing](https://namomarketing.com/justdial-vs-google-business-profile-india/)). Opinion pieces say it stays trusted in tier-2 and tier-3 towns, with no traffic data either way ([Jatin Agarwal](https://thejatinagarwal.in/justdial-more-popular-than-google-indian-small-businesses/)). The free GBP is still the sensible first step, and keeping Justdial details identical helps AI answers.

## What a freelancer can honestly sell

GBP setup is a crowded, cheap market, advertised mostly from ₹999 to ₹7,000 one-time. The ChatGPT claim is free and takes minutes, so it cannot carry a fee by itself. Early AI-visibility (GEO) packages list ₹9,999 to ₹30,000 a month, aimed at clinics, D2C brands and firms with websites, with no evidence found of buyer demand or results ([HeyTrill](https://heytrill.com/geo-agency-india); [Siviiy](https://siviiy.com/services/ai-search-optimization)). A typical kirana shop will not pay that.

The defensible value is the work AI tools cannot do for themselves: verification handling, correct and consistent business data across Google, Justdial and the website, a review-collection routine, and a monthly check of what Google and ChatGPT say about the shop. My judgment: a bundle priced around ₹3,000 to ₹8,000 one-time plus ₹1,500 to ₹4,000 a month at the micro end is plausible, extrapolated from existing GBP prices and not an observed price. Three rules follow. First, never promise "rank in ChatGPT" or "more customers from ChatGPT", because OpenAI says claiming does not change visibility and no outcome data exists. Second, ChatGPT local listings and Business Agents are not available to Indian local shops now, so offer only website-based claims where they work. Third, the shop owner should hold ownership of the Google and ChatGPT accounts, not the freelancer (my recommendation). Also beware of fake "Google agent", "verification" and review-removal callers, a pattern flagged by Indian sellers ([ScamBhai](https://scambhai.com/scams/fake-review-removal)).

## Verdict: do Google first, claim ChatGPT second

**For a small Indian business, Google Business Profile wins today.** **For a freelancer, sell GBP setup plus data consistency, and treat ChatGPT as a free extra.** The probabilities below are labelled **my judgment**. They are opinion and not model output.

## Conclusion

The surprise is how little the huge ChatGPT audience matters to a corner shop right now. India may be OpenAI's biggest market, yet OpenAI's own rules put local profiles in the US and tie Indian eligibility to owning a website, which most small shops lack. The chatbot's referral traffic is a rounding error beside Google's. The better framing is that Google profile data is the base layer for Google's own AI, and clean data on a website, directories and reviews is what carries over to every assistant.

The next thing to watch is not the profile but the dashboard. If OpenAI opens Indian local listings and the mentions and clicks counters start to show real numbers, the 3% to 5% estimates above should move quickly. Until then, a cheap test costs nothing: open chatgpt.com/business-hub (URL per [Cydelavega](https://cydelavega.com/blog/businesses-in-chatgpt/), reported, not verified) with an Indian business, see whether "Add business" and the ID check work, and record what happens.

## Main data gaps

There is **no outcome data** for ChatGPT business profiles: the beta is four days old, so no case studies, traffic or lead figures exist. India availability is **unconfirmed** for Business Hub, agents and sponsored agents, and OpenAI publishes no country list or expansion dates. Most numbers come from vendors, agencies or search summaries: the SE Ranking, Conductor and SEOcrawl traffic shares, the BrightLocal and SOCi visibility studies, the Raveneye website sample, and all rupee prices (advertised, not paid). Official OpenAI and Google page text was read through search extracts and may be incomplete. There is no India-specific study of ChatGPT local sources, of AI accuracy in Hindi, of Google profile adoption among Indian shops, or of GBP suspension rates there. The 1.2 billion weekly-user figure and India's "largest market" statement are company claims seen through secondary sources. The Indian user figure of about 200 million is my arithmetic. The 46% "local intent" Google statistic dates from 2018 and was not used. The BrightLocal 6% to 45% figure is a US survey of "ever used".
