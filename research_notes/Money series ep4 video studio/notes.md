## 3. Competition

### Takeaway
Clients can make their own AI videos for about $6 to $50 a month. That caps what a plain generated video can sell for.

### Cited Findings
- HeyGen Creator about $24 (annual) to $29 per month, Pro $49, Business $149. REPORTED (third-party, conflicting) — [HeyGen blog](https://www.heygen.com/blog/runway-pricing), [creatify](https://creatify.ai/blog/heygen-pricing-%282026%29-plans-and-what-you-ll-actually-pay)
- Runway entry about $12/month annual, billed by seconds of footage. REPORTED — [costbench](https://www.costbench.com/compare/heygen-vs-runway/)
- Canva Pro India ₹500/month or ₹4,000/year (one Sep 2026 blog; older and conflicting figures exist). REPORTED, weak — [techmitra](https://techmitra.in/?p=24321)
- Presenter tools about $1.50 to $3.00 a finished minute (one estimate). REPORTED — [fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/ai-video-tool-pricing-answered)

### Inferences
- Price pressure is high: self-serve tools cost less than one reel from a freelancer. A studio must sell speed, local language, brand fit and variants, not "a video".

### Gaps

## 4. Rules

### Takeaway
Use human-made or licensed assets, commercial-cleared music only, and label realistic AI content. India now has binding labelling rules for synthetic media on platforms.

### Cited Findings
- US Copyright Office Part 2 (29 Jan 2025): human authorship required; purely AI output not protected; AI-assisted works judged case by case. REPORTED (law-firm summaries) — [Copyright Alliance](https://copyrightalliance.org/ai-report-part-2-copyrightability/)
- India: nothing found on authorship of AI output under the Copyright Act. ANI v OpenAI interim order 24 Jul 2026 refused injunction (about training, not output ownership); interim only. REPORTED — [Cyril Amarchand](https://corporate.cyrilamarchandblogs.com/2026/07/ani-v-open-ai-delhi-high-court-refuses-interim-injunction-in-landmark-ai-copyright-dispute/)
- India IT Rules amendment on "synthetically generated information": notified 10 Feb 2026, effective 20 Feb 2026; intermediaries must label SGI clearly and prominently; faster takedowns. A further proposal on continuous labels may not be final. REPORTED (law-firm and press) — [Khaitan](https://www.khaitanco.com/thought-leadership/MeitY-notifies-the-IT-Amendment-Rules-2026), [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/story/govt-tightens-rules-on-ai-generated-content-mandates-labels-and-faster-takedowns-515505-2026-02-10)
- Music: Zee v Nykaa (Delhi High Court), claim about ₹2 crore over 12 reels; platform music licences are personal use; brands need sync licence or the Instagram Sound Collection or a commercial library. Case ongoing. REPORTED — [SpicyIP May 2026](https://spicyip.com/2026/05/zee-v-nykaa-the-210000-worth-instagram-reels.html), [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/licensing-syndication/1790610/zee-v-nykaa-india-what-every-brand-and-their-counsel-needs-to-know-about-music-on-instagram)
- YouTube asks creators to disclose realistic altered or synthetic content; label shown on video and in Shorts. REPORTED (2024 coverage, details may have changed) — [Android Central](https://androidcentral.com/apps-software/youtube-ai-synthetic-content-disclosure)
- Meta "AI info" labels via C2PA metadata (industry blog). REPORTED, weak — [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/ai-disclosure)

### Gaps
- Indian rules for doctor and clinic ads (NMC), coaching ad claims (ASCI, consumer authority), and health claims were NOT researched. Check before any clinic or coaching job.
- Platform AI model licence terms (Picsart model outputs, commercial use) not read.
