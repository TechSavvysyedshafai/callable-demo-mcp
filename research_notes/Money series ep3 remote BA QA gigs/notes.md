## Fees, tax, payments, scams
- Upwork: flat 10% replaced by variable 0 to 15% per contract from 1 May 2025, fixed once contract starts (blogs; help page not opened) — [Upwork help](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062538), [goLance](https://golance.com/blogs/upwork-fees-explained-2026)
- Freelancer.com $5 or 10% whichever greater (one outlier 3%); Contra 0% freelancer side — [GigRadar](https://gigradar.io/blog/best-upwork-alternatives)
- GST: Rs 20 lakh services threshold (Rs 10 lakh special category states); exports zero-rated with LUT; GSTR-1 and 3B still filed if registered — [Incorpx](https://www.incorpx.io/blog/gst-export-of-services-freelancers-india), [Xflow](https://www.xflowpay.com/blog/gst-for-freelancers). Sources conflict on whether unregistered freelancers can file LUT. Government pages not read.
- Section 44ADA: 50% deemed profit, receipts cap Rs 50 lakh (Rs 75 lakh if cash is 5% or less) — [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/section-44ada)
- Payments on a $3,000 invoice (Skydo's own comparison): Skydo $34, Payoneer $60, Wise $61, Stripe $223, PayPal $261; Skydo fee $19/$29 + 18% GST — [Skydo](https://www.skydo.com/blog/best-international-payment-platforms-for-freelancers-india)
- Scams: fee-for-job and refundable deposit pattern; report at 1930 and cybercrime.gov.in (blogs, official advisory text not found) — [Internshala](https://internshala.com/blog/job-scams/), [TrueJobs](https://truejobs.co.in/guides/how-to-spot-remote-job-scams)
