## 1. Audience scale: ChatGPT weekly users vs Google Search and Google Maps (global and India)

### Takeaway
ChatGPT reportedly reached roughly 1B to 1.2B weekly users globally in 2026. OpenAI's India head says India (about 100M weekly users in Feb 2026, since doubled) is now ChatGPT's largest market. Google Search still handles 5T+ queries per year, Gemini and AI Mode each report 1B+ monthly users, and Maps has "hundreds of millions" of monthly users with no verified India count.

### Cited Findings
- ChatGPT global, 27 Feb 2026: OpenAI said ChatGPT had more than 900M weekly active users. OFFICIAL as reported by secondary sources. — [Search summary of OpenAI-linked coverage](https://thenextweb.com/news/chatgpt-1-billion-weekly-active-users-openai-target)
- ChatGPT global, mid-2026: about 1B weekly users in July 2026, attributed to an OpenAI spokesperson speaking to The Verge. The date of the 1B milestone differs by source (July vs August 2026). — [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/chatgpt-1-billion-weekly-active-users-openai-target); [AI Magazine](https://aimagazine.com/news/openais-billion-users-us-852bn-valuation-and-stargate)
- ChatGPT global, late Sept/Oct 2026: 1.2B weekly users, reportedly told to developers at DevDay on 29 Sep 2026. I found no primary OpenAI page. Treat as reported, not confirmed. — [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-chatgpt-12b-weekly-users-devday-2026-distribution/)
- ChatGPT India, Feb 2026: Sam Altman said India had over 100M weekly active users, second only to the US, at the India AI Impact Summit. OFFICIAL as reported. — [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-india-100-million-weekly-chatgpt-users/)

### Inferences
- Weekly users (ChatGPT) and monthly users or annual queries (Google) are different units, so they do not compare directly. A fair framing is that ChatGPT's reach is now the same order of magnitude as Gemini, AI Mode and Maps users, but far smaller in query volume than Google Search (5T+ per year).
- India is now ChatGPT's biggest market. A rough India figure is 200M weekly users, assuming "doubled since January" applies to the roughly 100M base reported in Feb. This is my arithmetic, not a company figure.
- Google Maps' India user base is probably in the hundreds of millions, but I have no verified figure.

### Gaps

- No official 2026 Google Maps monthly users (global or India) and no official Google Search users in India.
- No newer Google annual query count than "5 trillion+" (Jan 2025).

## 2. Local intent: share of Google queries and of ChatGPT prompts that are shopping, local or "near me"

### Takeaway
The commonly quoted "46% of Google searches have local intent" is a 2018 Google statement, so it is stale. The best 2026 data is survey-based: BrightLocal (July 2026, US) shows 45% of consumers now use ChatGPT or generative AI for local business recommendations, up from 6% in 2025. OpenAI's own usage study found shopping or purchasing conversations are only about 2% of ChatGPT use.

### Cited Findings
- 46% of Google queries have local intent: a claim by a Google official in 2018, repeated by BrightLocal and many 2026 roundups without the date. Stale. — [BrightLocal local SEO statistics](https://brightlocal.com/reports/local-seo-statistics/); [QuickSEO](https://quickseo.ai/blog/local-seo-in-2026-46-percent-of-searches-have-local-intent-50-more-stats-smbs-should-know)
- "Near me" use: BrightLocal's 2025 survey says 46% of consumers "always" or "often" add "near me" to local searches. SURVEY (US). — [BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/insights/local-seo-statistics/)
- BrightLocal 2026 survey (run July 2026, 1,227 US consumers who searched for a local business in the prior 3 months): use of ChatGPT or other generative AI for local business recommendations rose from 6% (2025) to 45% (2026). Google's share of review-platform usage fell from 83% to 71%. Apple Maps rose from 14% to 27%. 73% of local searches start on mobile. 97% read reviews. SURVEY, US only, and I did not open the original. — [BrightLocal local SEO statistics](https://brightlocal.com/reports/local-seo-statistics/); [Digital Applied](https://digitalapplied.com/blog/local-seo-statistics/)
- "Near me" search volume estimates conflict: 200M or more per month (Glimpse, early 2026) vs about 800M per month for the US (2024 estimate). Neither is from Google. VENDOR-ESTIMATE. — [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/blog/local-seo-statistics)
- OpenAI's "How People Use ChatGPT" study (NBER working paper with David Deming, about 1.5M conversations): conversations about purchasing products were about 2.1% of all ChatGPT conversations. I could not confirm this on OpenAI's page, only through a secondary source. Shopping sits under the "seeking information" category. It is a share of conversations, not of messages. This is the Sept 2025 study, and I found no 2026 update. — [Yahoo Tech summary](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/articles/openai-just-shared-people-really-073757816.html); [OpenAI study page](https://openai.com/index/how-people-are-using-chatgpt/)
- Shopping ranks seventh among top UK ChatGPT uses, which suggests it is fairly low globally. — [TechRadar](https://techradar.com/ai-platforms-assistants/chatgpt/openai-reveals-chatgpts-most-popular-features-and-the-top-one-might-surprise-you)
- India-specific: OpenAI says India leads global ChatGPT use for coding and data analysis. I found no India-specific share of local or "near me" prompts. — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/tech/india-leads-global-chatgpt-use-for-coding-and-data-analysis-openai)
- Consumers start local searches on Google, then scatter across other platforms. — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/consumers-starting-local-searches-on-google--then-scattering)

### Inferences
- The 45% BrightLocal figure is "ever used AI for local recommendations", not "most local discovery". It is a reach figure, and probably far above the share of local queries that actually go to ChatGPT. The OpenAI study suggests only a few percent of ChatGPT use is shopping or purchasing.
- The two datasets together imply that many consumers try ChatGPT for local discovery, but Google (Search plus Maps) is still the default for intent-heavy local queries. This is my inference.

### Gaps
- No current official Google figure for the share of queries that are local or "near me". The 46% figure dates from 2018.
- No share of ChatGPT prompts that are local or "near me" specifically. The 2.1% figure covers purchasable products only.
- No India-specific local-intent data from BrightLocal, Pew, SparkToro or Semrush found. Pew and SparkToro came up empty in my searches.

## 3. Referral traffic from AI chatbots vs Google, and conversion quality

### Takeaway
AI chatbots send a tiny slice of website traffic: about 0.3% to 1.1% in 2026 studies, versus organic search at around 43% of traffic (SE Ranking). ChatGPT is about 75% to 83% of AI referrals. Conversion-quality claims range from worse than organic to 23x better. They come mostly from vendors, and none isolates local businesses.

### Cited Findings
- SE Ranking (2026): AI referral traffic was 0.32% of total website traffic, up from 0.24% in 2025 and 0.02% in 2024. ChatGPT's share of AI referrals was 74.78% in 2026 (79.74% in 2025). Organic search was 42.75% of traffic, so Google sent about 134x more visitors than AI platforms. VENDOR-ESTIMATE. — [SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/blog/ai-traffic-study/)
- Conductor 2026 AEO/GEO Benchmarks: AI referrals were 1.08% of website traffic across 13,770 domains and 3.3B sessions. VENDOR-ESTIMATE. — [Remote Work Europe summary](https://remoteworkeurope.eu/news/2026/conductor-aeo-geo-benchmarks-ai-referrals-one-percent)
- SEOcrawl (16,000+ sites): AI share of sessions rose from 0.23% to 0.36%, reaching 0.42% in Aug 2026. ChatGPT's share of AI referral sessions rose from 78% to 83% between March and Aug 2026. Much AI-driven traffic is not recorded as AI. — [SEOcrawl](https://seocrawl.ai/blog/ai-traffic-vs-organic-search-study)
- Similarweb (June 2025): about 1.13B referral visits from AI platforms vs about 191B from Google Search. Older, but the ratio is consistent with the 2026 studies. — [Search Engine Journal on Similarweb](https://searchenginejournal.com/ai-search-isnt-replacing-google-its-layering-on-top-similarweb-data/583378/)
- Ahrefs: I found no standalone Ahrefs referral-share study. An Ahrefs claim that AI referrals were 0.5% of its own traffic but 12.1% of its signups (23x) appears in a roundup. This is Ahrefs' own site, so it is not representative. — [Marketix Digital](https://marketixdigital.com.au/blog/ai-search-visitors-convert-up-to-23x-better-than-organic-traffic/)
- Conversion claims, mixed: Semrush about 4.4x (largely B2B topics); Seer Interactive about 9x (one client site); Microsoft Clarity 11x sign-ups for publisher and news sites (earlier 1.66% vs 0.15%). Opposite finding: ChatGPT traffic converted 13% worse than organic in one study, and a Similarweb-based figure of 7% vs 5% shows a small edge. — [Dev.to roundup](https://dev.to/mr_manushukla/ai-search-converts-23x-44x-or-215x-what-the-2026-studies-mean-for-your-geo-budget-5h0k); [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/seo/why-chatgpt-traffic-converts-worse-than-google-search/); [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/why-every-ai-search-study-tells-a-different-story-465511)
- Local-specific: Search Engine Land cautions that a local service provider experiences AI search very differently from SaaS or ecommerce. A SOCi 2026 Local Visibility Index claim ("AI 30x more selective than Google") could not be verified. — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/why-every-ai-search-study-tells-a-different-story-465511)
- Low-quality source, do not use: upgrowth.in claims AI drives 12% to 18% of referral traffic, with no method. — [upgrowth.in](https://upgrowth.in/ai-traffic-share-report-2026)

### Inferences
- Even with 10x better conversion, AI referrals at 0.3% to 1.1% of traffic would add little compared with Google organic at roughly 40%+ of traffic. For most small businesses, Google brings far more customers today.
- Referral counts understate AI influence. Customers may see a name in ChatGPT and then search Google or Maps, or phone directly. This is especially likely for local businesses, which are often reached through calls, directions and walk-ins that are not web referrals.
- No India-specific referral data was found.

### Gaps
- No local-business conversion study. No Ahrefs or Conductor India data. I could not open Similarweb to get 2026 referral counts.

## 4. Trend: is AI search growing at Google's expense? (2026 data)

### Takeaway
Evidence so far points to "layering, not replacing". Google reports its fastest query growth in five years, while ChatGPT grows fast but its share of AI web traffic is falling to Gemini and Claude. Google's search revenue growth slowed through 2026 (19%, 17%, 12%).

### Cited Findings
- Alphabet: Search and other revenue growth was 19% in Q1, 17% in Q2 and 12% in Q3 2026 (about $63.3B). Pichai said Q3 had the highest query growth in five years. Analysts note the odd pairing of record queries and flat sequential revenue, and that Google gives no AI Overviews or AI Mode click-through or revenue split. Alphabet shares fell about 8% after the Q3 report, per one report. — [Search Engine Journal (Q1)](https://searchenginejournal.com/google-search-revenue-grew-19-in-q1-pichai-cites-ai/573378/); [PPC Land (Q2)](https://ppc.land/google-search-sales-gain-just-79m-on-q2-as-query-growth-hits-5-year-high/); [Digit (Q3)](https://www.digit.in/news/general/googles-investments-in-ai-are-driving-more-searches-sundar-pichai-reveals-during-q3-2026-earnings-call.html)
- Similarweb: ChatGPT's share of generative-AI web traffic fell from about 77.6% to 53.7% over a year (Gemini 7.3% to 26.7%). Other snapshots: 64.5% in early Jan 2026 and 61.7% in Feb 2026, and 52.7% with Claude tripling in another report. These are web-only and exclude apps. — [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/chatgpts-web-traffic-share-dropped-from-78-to-54-in-one-year-as-gemini-quietly-tripled-its-reach/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/chatgpt-drops-to-52-7-as-claude-triples-its-ai-traffic-share); [Technobezz](https://www.technobezz.com/news/chatgpt-web-traffic-fell-22-percent-as-googles-gemini-captur-2026-01-08-bjfb)
- By Sept 2026, Gemini's share had fallen for a fourth straight month as ChatGPT regained ground (Similarweb). — [OfficeChai](https://officechai.com/ai/geminis-traffic-share-falls-for-fourth-straight-month-as-chatgpt-regains-ground-similarweb-data/)
- Similarweb framing: ChatGPT adds to, rather than replaces, Google use. One analysis found organic search traffic down only about 2.5% year on year. — [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/similarweb-finds-chatgpt-adds-to-rather-than-replaces-google-1e2ad8c8); [Leap.de](https://leap.de/en/wissen/fachartikel/chatgpt-verliert-an-einfluss-google-stabilisiert-sich-wie-sich-das-suchverhalten-2026-schon-jetzt-verandert)
- BrightLocal (July 2026, US): Google's share of consumer review-platform usage fell from 83% to 71%, while AI tool use for local recommendations rose from 6% to 45%. This is the clearest local-specific sign of erosion. SURVEY. — [BrightLocal](https://brightlocal.com/reports/local-seo-statistics/)

### Inferences
- Google is not losing volume. Its own surfaces (AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini) are absorbing the AI shift. It is losing some local-research share (BrightLocal) and has an unexplained revenue-per-query question.
- India is where ChatGPT is growing fastest, so the India local-discovery shift could come sooner than elsewhere. This is speculation and no data supports it directly.

### Gaps
- No India-specific trend data on Google vs ChatGPT usage. No independent audited ChatGPT user counts.
