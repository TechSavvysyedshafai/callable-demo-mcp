# Small-business economics and risks of AI-assistant listings (ChatGPT, Google AI Mode/Gemini) vs Google Business Profile: India focus and freelance setup services

Research date: 14 Nov 2026. Method note: WebFetch could not reach venturebeat.com, brightlocal.com or zarla.com (host not on the session's network allowlist), and Firecrawl is disconnected. Findings below come from Exa search/fetch excerpts and WebSearch snippets. Most items were read as page excerpts, not whole documents. Labels: [VERIFIED] = primary or official source read directly; [REPORTED] = secondary or vendor source, figures not independently checked; [ESTIMATE] = survey or vendor estimate with weak or unclear method.

## 1. How many Indian MSMEs and small shops exist, and what share have a Google Business Profile or website?

### Takeaway
The registered base is huge but mostly informal micro units (about 10 crore Udyam plus Udyam Assist registrations by 13 Nov 2026, of which about 4.5 crore are informal units). Reliable data on how many have a Google Business Profile is missing. The best indicators say roughly half of MSMEs use some digital tool, only about 20-27% have their own website, and only about 18% of map-listed independent small businesses have a real website.

### Cited Findings
- [VERIFIED] MSME Dashboard (real-time, as on 13 Nov 2026): total 10,11,41,207 (about 10.1 crore) registrations: Udyam 5,58,39,666 and Udyam Assist Platform (UAP, informal micro units) 4,53,01,541. Micro 10,05,52,951, small 5,46,151, medium 42,105. Trading 4.20 crore, services 3.93 crore, manufacturing 1.98 crore. — [MSME Dashboard](https://dashboard.msme.gov.in/)
- [VERIFIED] PIB factsheet (6 Oct 2026): about 9.78 crore Udyam + UAP registrations; female-owned 37.6%. Lok Sabha answer (30 Jul 2026): 8,84,20,556 cumulative registrations 1 Jul 2020 to 30 Jun 2026. — [PIB factsheet](https://www.pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?ModuleId=16&NoteId=151082&id=151082&lang=1&reg=44); [Lok Sabha Q1892](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AU1892_Vlih8E.pdf)
- [VERIFIED] The registration count is not the same as the count of operating businesses. UAP covers informal micro enterprises without PAN/GST. The Economic Survey cites 6.3 crore unincorporated non-agricultural enterprises (NSS 73rd round, FY16). Another survey found only about 12.7 million of 51.5 million Udyam-registered MSMEs are GST registered (India SME Forum / Amazon Business, via search summary, [REPORTED]). — [The Hindu on Economic Survey](https://www.thehindu.com/business/msmes-continue-to-face-challenges-in-formalisation-and-accessing-credit-economic-survey/article68432203.ece)
- [REPORTED] India SME Forum / Meta report (Dec 2025; 7,835 MSMEs, a convenience sample "indicative rather than statistically representative", 91% of respondents aged 45-54): 53.8% adopted at least one digital tool, 46.2% fully offline; own website 26.9% in the digitally active cohort vs 20-25% quoted as national average for registered MSMEs. The report quotes Economic Survey 2024-25 (55-60% of registered MSMEs online "in some form") and NASSCOM-Deloitte MSME Digital Index 2025 (57% digitally engaged). I did not check those two sources directly. — [India SME Forum / Meta report card](https://indiasmeforum.org/digishaastra/assets/docs/Final-META-Report-Card-2025.pdf)
- [ESTIMATE] Raveneye (a vendor) measured 66,452 independent Indian small businesses sampled from Google Business Profile map listings in 55 cities and 22 trades (snapshot late July 2026): only 18.4% had their own website; of the sites, 59.0% had no structured data; about 1.2% of map-listed businesses were fully machine-readable (vs about 32.1% in its US edition). The sample is already biased toward businesses with a map profile. — [Raveneye](https://raveneyeglobal.com/research/small-business-ai-visibility-india-2026-07/)
- [REPORTED] Google/India SME Forum "Google Dividend" report (survey of 3,249 MSMEs, June 2026, Google-aligned): Google says 16 million+ businesses are discoverable via its platforms (Google Business Profile, Merchant Centre) and 23 million+ merchants transact via GPay; 41% of surveyed MSMEs report improved local discovery. Vendor-friendly source. — [Google Dividend report](https://indiasmeforum.org/high-level-dialogue/uploads/Report_Google.pdf)
- [REPORTED] Older Google data: KPMG/Google 2017 study (about 51 million SMEs; 32% digitally engaged, per Rajan Anandan 2017) and a 2019 Google statement of 26 million listings, about 58.5 million SMEs, 45% present on Google Maps/Search. Both are dated and come from Google speakers. — [The Hans India 2019](https://www.thehansindia.com/business/smes-fast-adopting-new-tech-google-india-541472); [The Better India 2017](https://thebetterindia.com/112396/going-small-large-internet-googles-rajan-anandan/)
- [ESTIMATE] Small academic samples: a Maharashtra survey of 150 micro businesses found only 24% aware of Google Business Profile and about 10% actively managing a listing. A survey of 201 MSMEs found 51% rated Google Business Profile among the "most useful" platforms, behind Instagram (83%), Facebook (77%) and WhatsApp Business (69%). — [Maharashtra study](https://www.eelet.org.uk/index.php/journal/article/view/3053); [IJSREM study](https://doi.org/10.55041/ijsrem51165)
- [REPORTED] Zinnov (2020, via BW Disrupt): of 75 million MSMEs, 16-18 million have a social presence, online listing or website. Dated. — [BW Disrupt](https://www.bwdisrupt.com/article/one-third-of-msmes-enhance-digital-presence-during-lockdown-reports-290994)
- [VERIFIED] Google says it grounds Maps/Gemini features on about 35 million places in India (Nov 2025). — [Google India blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/explore-communicate/google-maps-in-india-supercharged-with-gemini/)

### Inferences
- The addressable base for a GBP-setup service is plausibly several million to tens of millions of shops. The data does not give a clean number. Treat "10 crore MSMEs" as a registration count, mostly informal solo units that may never need a Google profile.
- Indian small businesses are messaging-first (WhatsApp, Instagram) and website-poor, so a ChatGPT listing route that requires a website (see section 5) excludes most of them today.

### Gaps
- No credible, independent figure for the share of Indian small shops with a claimed Google Business Profile. Google's numbers are listing counts (including unclaimed/auto-created) and come from Google or Google-aligned reports. I did not locate the Bain/Meta 2025 "MSME" report text beyond a WhatsApp Business/Bain page (650 million Indians on social/messaging, about 200 million shop online, [REPORTED](https://whatsappbusiness.com/resources/resource-library/win-with-conversations-bain-india/)).
- KPMG/Google primary 2026 data not found.

## 2. How AI assistants choose which businesses to recommend, and does a strong Google profile feed ChatGPT?

### Takeaway
Google's AI surfaces (AI Mode, AI Overviews, Ask Maps/Gemini) lean heavily on Google Business Profile. ChatGPT leans on Yelp, directories and business websites, and uses Google Business Profile much less (US/UK/Australia data; no India-specific study found). So a strong Google profile helps with Google's AI surfaces directly and with ChatGPT only indirectly, via the website, reviews and consistent data. Overall AI visibility is far lower than Google local-pack visibility.

### Cited Findings
- [REPORTED] BrightLocal (Sep 2026; 200,085 localized prompts, about 1,300 US/UK/AU locations, 1.9 million citations, vendor tracker): Google Maps mentioned a tracked business in 66% of searches vs 32-38% for AI surfaces (AI Overviews 38%, ChatGPT 33%, AI Mode 32%). Same prompt repeated returns only 20-33% overlapping result sets (ChatGPT 23%). Businesses recur about half the time. Google Business Profile = 28.5% of all citations; Yelp about 9.5%; business websites = 42% of citations and 93% of unique cited domains. ChatGPT overlaps only 19-20% with Google's AI surfaces. "While Google Business Profile is important for AI Mode and AIO, it's hardly used by ChatGPT." — [BrightLocal AI local visibility study](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-ai-visibility-study/)
- [REPORTED] BrightLocal (Aug 2026; 60,970 checks, US/UK/AU): for ChatGPT, Yelp was a source in 80% of answers (99,281 citations); Tripadvisor, OpenTable, BBB next; Apple was the only major map-type source. Advice: claim both Google Business Profile and Yelp, keep listings updated, get into general and industry directories. — [BrightLocal directory sources](https://www.brightlocal.com/resources/ai-directory-sources/)
- [REPORTED] BrightLocal (Dec 2024; 800 US ChatGPT searches): business websites 58% of sources, business mentions 27%, directories 15%; Yelp, Facebook and Google Maps did not appear as directory sources then. Dated, and differs from the 2026 finding, so ChatGPT's source mix has shifted. — [BrightLocal 2024](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/uncovering-chatgpt-search-sources/)
- [VERIFIED as reported by Search Engine Land] SOCi 2026 Local Visibility Index (about 350,000 locations, 2,751 multi-location brands, mostly US chains): ChatGPT recommended 1.2% of locations, Perplexity 7.4%, Gemini 11%; Google 3-pack showed 35.9%. SOCi estimates AI visibility is 3-30x harder. Profile information was about 68% accurate on ChatGPT/Perplexity vs 100% on Gemini (grounded in Google Maps). Locations ChatGPT recommended averaged 4.3 stars (Gemini 3.9, Perplexity 4.1); weak-rated brands (about 3.4 stars, review response under 5%) were effectively invisible. Fewer than half of Google-local leaders also led in AI. Vendor study, chains only. — [Search Engine Land, 28 Jan 2026](https://searchengineland.com/ai-local-visibility-report-2026-468085)
- [VERIFIED] Google Ask Maps (Gemini) launched 12 Mar 2026 in the US and India; Hindi added 6 Aug 2026 (per U Scale, an Indian agency, [REPORTED]). Google says it draws on over 300 million places and reviews from 500 million contributors, and has not said how it ranks businesses; no ads in Ask Maps at launch, and Google declined to say whether paid placement will come. — [Search Engine Journal, 12 Mar 2026](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-maps-launches-ai-conversational-search-with-ask-maps/569585/); [U Scale](https://uscale.in/blog/ask-maps-google-maps-india)
- [VERIFIED] OpenAI says that, apart from claimed information, "ChatGPT may also use other sources when answering questions about your business", and claiming "doesn't change its visibility". OpenAI says paying/advertising does not influence answers. — [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt); [VentureBeat, 10 Nov 2026](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-lets-you-claim-your-business-in-chatgpt-and-set-up-a-custom-agentic-ai-customer-sales-rep)
- [REPORTED] Storefront locations were mentioned more than service-area businesses (33.6% vs 29.4% average). — [BrightLocal study](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-ai-visibility-study/)
- [REPORTED] Honest-limits note: several "GEO" claims in agency pages (e.g., structured data or llms.txt raising visibility "by up to 40%") trace to vendor pages. I did not verify a peer-reviewed source and did not find an independent study proving llms.txt effects. — e.g. [MarketingPulse](https://www.marketingpulse.in/seo-services/generative-engine-optimization)

### Inferences
- Answer to "does a strong Google profile feed ChatGPT?": partly and indirectly. Google's own AI surfaces use Google Business Profile as the top source. ChatGPT mostly cites Yelp, directories and the business's website (US/UK/AU evidence). The things that carry across all of them are: accurate, consistent name/address/phone/hours, a real website, and good review ratings/volume.
- In India, Yelp is thin and Justdial, IndiaMART, Zomato, Swiggy and Practo matter more (my assumption). I found no study of which sources ChatGPT uses for Indian local queries.
- Because ChatGPT's local picks are volatile (about 23% repeat overlap), a one-time "listing" is unlikely to produce stable visibility. Any freelancer promising "rank in ChatGPT" is overpromising.

### Gaps
- No India-specific study of ChatGPT or AI Mode local-recommendation sources (all large studies are US/UK/AU).
- No independent, peer-reviewed GEO study isolated here; most are vendor studies (BrightLocal, SOCi, Raveneye).
- Review-count vs recommendation correlation for Indian businesses: not found.

## 3. What Indian agencies and freelancers charge: Google Business Profile, and any early market for AI-visibility (ChatGPT) setup

### Takeaway
GBP setup in India is a low-ticket, commoditised service: roughly ₹1,000 to ₹7,000 one-time, with monthly management about ₹700 to ₹3,500 at the low end and ₹6,999 to ₹18,000+ per location at larger agencies. An early GEO/AI-visibility market already exists, with agency pages listing about ₹9,999 to ₹30,000 per month for SMB tiers (up to lakhs for enterprise), but I found no evidence of independent buyer demand or outcomes, and the ChatGPT-claim step itself is free.

- GBP setup is price-compressed (race to ₹1,000-₹5,000) and the ChatGPT claim is free and takes minutes, so the defensible value is verification handling, content/data consistency, review systems and ongoing monitoring, not the claim itself.
- Current GEO list prices (₹9,999+ per month) are above what a typical kirana-scale shop would pay. They target clinics, D2C and service firms with a website.

### Gaps
- No transaction-level evidence (invoices, buyer surveys) of what Indian small shops actually pay; all prices are advertised.
- No evidence found on how many Indian small businesses have bought any GEO service or on churn.
- Freelancer.com and Upwork rate data limited to two search snippets.

## 4. Risks: wrong information, fake reviews, lock-in, privacy, ad costs, Hindi/language support

### Takeaway
Wrong or stale facts (hours, phone) are measurable but usually traced to inconsistent source data the business controls. Review extortion and fake-review fraud are documented on Google Maps, with Indian-origin actors reported. Platform dependence is high, and ChatGPT ads are now sold in India from ₹725/day. Language support exists for Hindi on Google (AI Mode, Ask Maps) and is expanding on ChatGPT, but local-business accuracy in Hindi is untested in any study I found.

### Cited Findings
Wrong information
- [REPORTED] Insites (Jul 2026; 10,000 US businesses, GBP as reference): ChatGPT's details fully matched Google Business Profile in 55.6% of cases (Perplexity 48.1%); wrong phone 30.1%, wrong website 15.6%, wrong name 6.4%, no match at all 1.7% for ChatGPT. Caveat from the authors: call-tracking numbers inflate "wrong phone". — [Insites](https://insites.com/blog/does-chatgpt-hallucinate-local-businesses-the-data-on-ai-accuracy/)
- [REPORTED] Suff Digital (Sep 2026; US): 14.4% of businesses AI described as open 24/7 do not list 24-hour hours on Google; only 1.4% of ChatGPT listings had phone mismatches vs Google. — [Suff Digital](https://www.suffdigital.com/resources/data-studies/ai-business-hours-wrong)
- [REPORTED] Herringbone (Sep 2026; 600 US small businesses): 27% of Google AI Overviews answers and 20% of ChatGPT answers contained a detail the business does not publish; of 59 businesses with no hours on their site, 46 had set Google to "Open 24 hours". — [Herringbone](https://herringbone.com/does-ai-get-your-business-details-right/)
- [REPORTED] sbw studio (Oct 2026; 12 Australian businesses, tiny sample): 224 of 238 facts right; hours wrong for 5 of 12; addresses and phones never wrong. Koozai (UK, Nov 2026): ChatGPT and Google AI Mode each recommended permanently closed restaurants in a test; 66% of picks differed between platforms. — [sbw studio](https://sbw.studio/en/journal/chatgpt-wrong-about-business/); [Portsmouth News](https://www.portsmouth.co.uk/your-world/chatgpt-and-google-ai-recommend-closed-portsmouth-restaurants-in-new-dining-experiment-9338139)
- [VERIFIED] Google itself warns "AI-powered answers can sometimes be incorrect" for Ask Maps (as quoted by U Scale). — [U Scale](https://uscale.in/blog/ask-maps-google-maps-india)
Fake reviews / extortion
- [VERIFIED] Google Business Profile Help documents a "Merchant Extortion" report form for review-extortion scams (sudden 1-2 star waves then payment demand); do not pay. — [Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/16404809?hl=en)
- [REPORTED] NYT/AP (via Economic Times, 18 Sep 2025): fraudsters extort businesses by posting or threatening fake negative reviews; a reviewer-for-hire quoted $100 for 20 reviews; Google says it removes most fraudulent content and restricted 900,000+ accounts; no direct way to speak to Google. El Pais (Jan 2026): Google says it blocked/removed 240 million+ reviews and 12 million+ fake business profiles since 2024. TOI (2022) cited an NYT report that many one-star-review scammers were in India. Indian SME press (Sep 2025) reports local cases (café/restaurant WhatsApp and UPI demands; evidence mixed, not independently verified here). — [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/small-biz/sme-sector/small-businesses-face-a-new-threat-pay-up-or-be-flooded-with-bad-reviews/articleshow/123965310.cms); [El Pais](https://english.elpais.com/technology/2026-01-24/this-is-how-extortion-campaigns-target-small-businesses-on-google-maps-they-can-ruin-you.html); [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/gadgets-news/how-google-wants-to-stop-the-one-star-review-scam-from-india/articleshow/92969165.cms); [SME Communities](https://smecommunities.com/reputation-on-ransom-why-small-businesses-must-tackle-review-extortion-head-on/)
- [REPORTED] A listed Indian scam pattern: callers offer "verification" or "review removal" for a fee or ask for your OTP/listing access; one listing advertised "remove 1-star, add 50 five-star reviews, Rs 7,500" (itself a policy violation). Another seller states it never sells fake reviews. — [ScamBhai](https://scambhai.com/scams/fake-review-removal); [Moodily](https://moodily.in/services/google-business-profile/)
Platform lock-in / control / privacy
- [VERIFIED] ChatGPT Business Hub: beta; free at launch but OpenAI left open future charging (Digiday via VentureBeat); owner needs a Persona identity check (government ID, business registration proof possible); OpenAI reviews and publishes edits; businesses do not see user prompts; insights only above thresholds, 28-day aggregates. Claiming does not change visibility. — [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt); [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-lets-you-claim-your-business-in-chatgpt-and-set-up-a-custom-agentic-ai-customer-sales-rep)
- [REPORTED] SE Roundtable author: "nervous about people hijacking businesses" on Business Hub; no data yet. An ownership "Dispute" flow exists. — [SE Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/chatgpt-business-hub-42505.html)
- [VERIFIED] Google's small-agency access model: agencies are added as managers (no password sharing) — a Nexify description; the risk of agency-held profile ownership is a general industry caution, not a sourced statistic. — [Nexify](https://www.nexify.co.in/gigs/google-business-profile-and-local-seo-setup)
Ad costs
- [VERIFIED/REPORTED] ChatGPT Ads launched in India end of Aug 2026 for Free and Go (₹399/month) users; self-serve Ads Manager from 4 Sep 2026, daily budgets from ₹725; ads not shown beside health, mental health or politics; WPP and Omnicom first agency partners; Razorpay partner (Oct). An earlier upGrowth article (Feb 2026) cited a US$200,000 minimum before India self-serve, which is now outdated. A Business Agent can be an ad destination in the US. — [Business Standard, 27 Aug 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/openai-chatgpt-ads-users-india-free-go-plans-chatbot-advertising-free-plans-126082700310_1.html); [Inc42, 2 Sep 2026](https://inc42.com/features/the-ad-break-comes-to-ai/); [Nakoda News](https://news.nakoda.ai/article/india-overtakes-us-chatgpt-largest-market-openai-ads-indian-brands)
- [REPORTED] Google's own survey claims MSME ad returns of 2-3x (top 5x) on ad spend; Google-aligned source. — [Google Dividend report](https://indiasmeforum.org/high-level-dialogue/uploads/Report_Google.pdf)
Language support
- [VERIFIED] Google AI Mode: English in India Jul 2025, Hindi Sep 2025, plus Bengali, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu Oct 2025. — [Google India blog (Hindi)](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/google-search-ai-mode-now-available-in-hindi/); [Google India blog (7 languages)](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/supercharging-search-for-india-new-languages-in-ai-mode-search-live-debuts/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/08/googles-search-live-comes-to-india-ai-mode-gets-more-languages/)
- [REPORTED] ChatGPT: India is now ChatGPT's largest market by weekly users (OpenAI India MD, ET, Nov 2026; earlier official figure over 100 million weekly users in Feb 2026); OpenAI added two Hindi voices (Oct 2026) and says it is prioritising Hindi and other Indian languages and entry-level Android phones; users aged 18-24 about half of Indian usage (per Altman, via Nakoda News). — [Economic Times video](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/corner-office-conversation/india-becomes-chatgpts-biggest-market-openais-india-chief-corner-office-conversation/videoshow/135651959.cms); [Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/info-tech/chatgpt-usage-doubles-in-india-over-past-year-openai-expands-hindi-voice-capabilities/article71589092.ece); [TechCrunch, Feb 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/15/india-has-100m-weekly-active-chatgpt-users-sam-altman-says/)

### Inferences
- Many "wrong info" cases come from the business's own inconsistent data (old hours, "24 hours" setting, directories). A paid setup service can reduce this risk by fixing the sources, which is a legitimate value proposition, but it cannot control what the AI says.
- The listing owner depends on a platform that has said it may charge later and reviews all edits. Keep ownership of the Google/ChatGPT accounts with the shop owner, not the freelancer (my recommendation, not a sourced statement).
- Review-extortion and fake-review risk exists regardless of AI, but AI answers that filter on star ratings (SOCi) could magnify damage from a review attack.

### Gaps
- No study on accuracy of AI answers about Indian local businesses, or in Hindi/Hinglish.
- No evidence on privacy incidents specific to Business Hub (new product).
- Google Business Profile suspension/hijack rates for Indian small businesses: no reliable statistic found.

## 5. Early reports on how businesses fared after claiming ChatGPT profiles

### Takeaway
Evidence is essentially nonexistent: Business Hub launched on 10 Nov 2026, four days before this research date. There are launch-day walkthroughs and speculation, no outcome data. Eligibility for Indian local shops is limited.

### Cited Findings
- [VERIFIED] Launch: OpenAI "Businesses in ChatGPT" beta announced 10 Nov 2026 (ChatGPT cited at 1.2 billion weekly users). Claim through Business Hub using phone, email, website domain (DNS) or Shopify; local stores and restaurants can currently claim only an existing US listing; Business Agents limited to eligible US ecommerce/online service businesses. — [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/); [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)
- [REPORTED] India: Indian businesses with a website can try to claim or create an online profile. Brilad (an Indian agency, 12 Nov) says a physical-location profile in a test led to a waitlist and that at least one non-US claim was waitlisted on day one (citing PPC Land); BananaBlocks (Indian agency) reports claiming in about five minutes via DNS and seeing a draft dashboard with dashes for mentions/clicks. Gadgets Now (14 Nov) says an Indian restaurant or neighbourhood store should not expect to create a local listing yet. — [Brilad](https://www.brilad.com/chatgpt-business-hub-how-to-claim-your-business-and-what-claiming-does-not-do/); [BananaBlocks](https://bananablockssolutions.in/chatgpt-business-hub-india/); [Gadgets Now](https://gadgetsnow.indiatimes.com/how-to/how-to-claim-your-business-on-chatgpt-steps-to-set-up-verify-profile-and-use-ai-agents/articleshow/135816378.cms)
- [REPORTED] The profile preview in India defaulted to ecommerce fields ("Delivery unavailable", "Returns not accepted") and the flow offered $500 in ad credit; the Agent builder tab appeared although publishing is not yet open outside the US (BananaBlocks, single anecdote).
- [VERIFIED] Dashboard: mentions count differently for local vs online businesses (local counts when a location appears in a result even if not opened). Metrics may be blank below thresholds. — [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)
- [REPORTED] BrightLocal says (Nov 2026) its earlier study found consumer use of generative AI as a source of local recommendations rose from 6% to 45% over a year; I did not locate the underlying study and this is a US-centric vendor claim. — [BrightLocal on Business Hub](https://www.brightlocal.com/learn/chatgpt-business-hub/)
