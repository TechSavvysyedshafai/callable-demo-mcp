# Businesses in ChatGPT (OpenAI, beta announced 10 Nov 2026): fact sheet as of 14 Nov 2026

Method note: direct WebFetch of openai.com, ecorpit.com and exchange4media.com failed (host not in the session's allowed network domains). Official OpenAI text below was retrieved through Exa search extracts of the official URLs, so wording is OpenAI's own. Each finding is tagged [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] (OpenAI page text) or [REPORTED] (press/third party). Treat [REPORTED] as unconfirmed by OpenAI.

## 1. What is claimable/free in Business Hub, and in which markets (is India included)?

### Takeaway
Business Hub (claim profile, edit details, product feed, Insights) is free at launch, and OpenAI says any eligible business "located where ChatGPT is available" can claim. OpenAI publishes no country list for Business Hub and does not name India. India is therefore neither confirmed nor excluded for profile claiming. Local stores and restaurants are US-only for now. Whether India works depends on whether a verification method (phone, business email, website domain, Shopify) and Persona ID check are supported there, which OpenAI does not say.

### Cited Findings
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] Announced 10 Nov 2026 as a beta. Business Hub lets businesses claim their business, manage information and see insights. OpenAI cites 1.2 billion weekly ChatGPT users. — [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/) (10 Nov 2026)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] "Any eligible business located where ChatGPT is available can claim their business in ChatGPT through Business Hub." Enterprise customers and advertisers can claim via their OpenAI representative. Shopify merchants can connect from Business Hub or Shopify admin. — [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/) (10 Nov 2026)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] Once verified, businesses can update key details and add self-service product feeds with pricing and availability. — [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/) (10 Nov 2026)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] Editable fields: website, business description, product or service category, logo, delivery policy, return policy, optional cover photo. OpenAI reviews and publishes changes; updates can take time to appear; "ChatGPT may also use other sources when answering questions about your business." — [OpenAI Help Center: Setting up your business on ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt) (undated, live 14 Nov 2026)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] Product feed can be Shopify, an uploaded CSV following OpenAI's product feed spec, or a hosted URL; feed products must follow Commerce policies. Connecting a feed "doesn't publish an agent or guarantee that ChatGPT recommends your products." — [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] Eligibility: must own the business or be authorized to represent it. No Ads account or ChatGPT Enterprise agreement needed. "Availability depends on the business's location and type." Claiming is "initially available to eligible businesses with a website." — [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt); [OpenAI Businesses in ChatGPT page](https://openai.com/business/businesses-in-chatgpt/)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] "Local stores and restaurants can currently claim only an existing U.S. listing." Eligible online businesses may see an option to add a profile. — [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] Eligibility depends on industry, location and verification/integrity checks. OpenAI may decline businesses with significant safety or integrity concerns and may hold off on other categories "until we have the safeguards needed." Eligibility "may change over time." — [OpenAI policy: Businesses in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/policies/businesses-in-chatgpt/) (updated 10 Nov 2026)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] OpenAI plans to expand Businesses in ChatGPT and Business Agents "to more markets and business categories" and to build tools for organizations with more complex needs. No dates given. — [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)
- [REPORTED] Digiday: Benji Shomair (OpenAI VP of monetization) "declined to share a timeline for further rollout." — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/openais-new-business-agents-give-brands-a-free-way-into-the-conversation-its-also-selling-as-an-ad/) (10 Nov 2026)
- [REPORTED] Price: Hub and agents are "free for now"; Shomair "didn't rule out a paid model in the future." — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/openais-new-business-agents-give-brands-a-free-way-into-the-conversation-its-also-selling-as-an-ad/) (10 Nov 2026)
- [REPORTED] India: one explainer states OpenAI does not publish a country list for Business Hub and that none of the sources it checked mention India; it infers online businesses may claim if verification works locally while local listings and agents stay US-only. This is that site's inference, not an OpenAI statement. — [EpiFive](https://www.epifive.com/blog/businesses-in-chatgpt-business-hub-business-agent) (12 Nov 2026)
- [REPORTED] PuppyIP: reviewed materials "give no complete country or industry list or full fee schedule." — [PuppyIP](https://puppyip.com/en/resources/businesses-in-chatgpt-profile-agent-eligibility-guide) (11 Nov 2026)
- Do not confuse with "ChatGPT Business" (paid team workspace plan); a subscription is unrelated to claiming. — [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt) (no Enterprise/Ads requirement); [Brandlight](https://brandlight.ai/blog/chatgpt-business-hub) (13 Nov 2026, [REPORTED])

### Inferences
- India profile claiming is plausible for online businesses with a website, since ChatGPT is available in India and no exclusion is stated, but this is unverified. Indian local shops/restaurants are almost certainly not claimable now (US listings only).
- The practical test is to open chatgpt.com/business-hub (URL per [Cydelavega](https://cydelavega.com/blog/businesses-in-chatgpt/), [REPORTED]) from an Indian business and see whether "Add business" and Persona verification work.

### Gaps
- No official country list for Business Hub; no confirmation or denial for India.
- No official fee schedule beyond "free at launch"; no statement on future paid tiers.
- Which verification methods work outside the US is not documented.

## 2. What do Business Agents do, who is eligible, pricing/fees?

### Takeaway
A Business Agent is a labeled, separate AI chat that answers questions about one business from information the business supplies. At launch it is limited to eligible US ecommerce and online-service businesses with a website, is free, and cannot take payments, bookings or check orders. Not available in India.

### Cited Findings
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] After claiming, eligible organizations "can build, test, and launch a Business Agent." When a business appears in a model response users can open its profile and, if an agent exists, start a conversation. The chat is "clearly labeled, separate from the customer's original ChatGPT conversation, and distinct from ChatGPT's independent answers." — [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/) (10 Nov 2026)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] "Business Agents are now available to eligible ecommerce and online service businesses in the United States." Requires a website and meeting industry requirements in the Business Agents Policy. — [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/); [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] Agents cannot complete purchases, make bookings or check a customer's order; they explain products/policies and link to the website. A business's ChatGPT plugin is not connected to the agent's chat. — [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] Setup: claim, then Persona identity verification approved, then create agent (or open Shopify-created draft), review info, preview with test questions, then press Publish. Publishing is a separate optional step; you cannot create an agent while verification is pending or failed. Shopify not required. — [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] Policy: agent must state it is AI representing the business (not human, independent adviser, or OpenAI-endorsed); no invented prices/offers/reviews; no false urgency or pressure; no unnecessary high-risk personal data (health, IDs, financial credentials); no submitting forms/bookings/purchases without explicit authorization via an approved feature; no licensed-professional advice; no guarantees. OpenAI considers business category and intended use before publication; "Business verification alone does not guarantee agent eligibility." May suspend/remove agents or restrict businesses. — [OpenAI Business Agents Policy](https://openai.com/policies/business-agents-policy/) (updated 10 Nov 2026)
- [REPORTED] Businesses cannot bring their own agent: "We aren't allowing folks to bring their own agents" (Shomair). Agent is built from supplied instructions, context documents and product feed. Shopper is handed to the brand's own site to pay. — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/openais-new-business-agents-give-brands-a-free-way-into-the-conversation-its-also-selling-as-an-ad/) (10 Nov 2026)
- [REPORTED] Agent payments: one source says OpenAI is building agent payments with Visa (attributed to The Next Web). Not confirmed in OpenAI text I could read. — [Dialogue Ads](https://dialogueads.com/guide/businesses-in-chatgpt) (11 Nov 2026)
- [REPORTED] Pricing: free (see section 1). Whether B2B software counts as an "online service" is unestablished. — [Saastrix](https://www.saastrix.uk/blog/how-to-get-your-business-on-chatgpt) (12 Nov 2026)

### Inferences
- Indian businesses should not expect agent access in the near term; US-only has no stated timeline.
- The agent is a closed, OpenAI-hosted sales assistant, not a transactional agent, so it is closer to an on-site FAQ bot than a booking/checkout agent.

### Gaps
- Full list of eligible categories (OpenAI links "eligible categories" but the text was not retrievable).
- Expansion timeline and future pricing.

## 3. How do ChatGPT Ads / Sponsored Agents work; pricing and availability?

### Takeaway
A Sponsored Business Agent is the paid version of the same agent: an ad shows "Start chat" and "Visit site"; no separate fee per chat, billed under the campaign. OpenAI publishes no price for sponsored agents; ChatGPT Ads generally are live in 89 countries including India (self-serve, billed legal entity must be in a listed country). Sponsored agents themselves are rolling out to "eligible advertisers", and original testing was with select US advertisers; India availability of the sponsored-agent format is not stated.

### Cited Findings
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] "Businesses can also sponsor their Business Agent and use it as the destination for ChatGPT Ads." Sponsored placements are clearly labeled and separate from ChatGPT responses. — [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/) (10 Nov 2026)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] Sponsored Business Agents are "rolling out to eligible advertisers in Ads Manager over the coming weeks." Built in Ads Manager > Business agent > Agent builder. Attach to a campaign with the Conversion optimization objective via "Enable agent chats"; publishing alone does not start delivery. Users see Start chat / Visit site. — [OpenAI Help Center: Sponsored Business Agent](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001651-setting-up-a-sponsored-business-agent-in-ads-manager)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] No separate charge per agent conversation or message; the campaign's billing settings apply. Advertisers get impressions and clicks; no transcripts; once an agent has more than 25 conversations an insights agent can help explore patterns. — [OpenAI Help Center: Sponsored Business Agent](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001651-setting-up-a-sponsored-business-agent-in-ads-manager)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] Business Hub agent and Ads Manager agent are not synced today; OpenAI says it is working on linking them. — [OpenAI Help Center: Sponsored Business Agent](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001651-setting-up-a-sponsored-business-agent-in-ads-manager)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] Sponsored Agents test began 16 Sep 2026 with select US advertisers: after a relevant ad a user can start a labeled conversation with a business-sponsored agent. — [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/09/16/openais-new-sponsored-agents-are-happy-to-chat-about-selling-you-things/5296946) (16 Sep 2026, quoting OpenAI's announcement; [REPORTED] as secondary)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] ChatGPT Ads: testing began in the US on 9 Feb 2026; ads show on Free and Go plans only (not Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, Edu; not under-18 accounts). Ads "do not influence ChatGPT's answers"; separate systems. Ads Manager is in beta; supports CPC bidding and conversion measurement. — [OpenAI Help Center: Ads in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001047-ads-in-chatgpt); [FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001220-frequently-asked-questions)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] 31 Aug 2026: ads reached $1bn annualized run rate in under 200 days; self-service Ads Manager launching across India, Europe, Middle East and North Africa. — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/expanding-access-to-ai-with-chatgpt-ads/) (31 Aug 2026)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] 21 Oct 2026: expansion to 89 countries; over 100,000 advertisers have run campaigns. — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-ads-expands-26-new-countries/) (21 Oct 2026)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] India is listed "Available" for self-service; the legal entity that advertises and is billed must be based in a listed country. New self-serve accounts may initially be limited to their home country. — [OpenAI Help Center: Ads Manager Availability](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001245-ads-manager-availability); [Creating campaigns](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001210-creating-campaigns-for-chatgpt-ads)
- [REPORTED] India launch: ChatGPT Ads launched in India for logged-in adults on Free and Go via WPP and Omnicom; self-serve from 4 Sep; daily budgets from Rs 725. Note date conflict: Hindu BusinessLine reports 4 Sep for self-serve (27 Aug article), while OpenAI's own post dated 31 Aug says self-serve launches "later today" in India. — [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/info-tech/openai-launches-chatgpt-ads-in-india-for-free-and-go-users/article71395054.ece) (27 Aug 2026); contradicted on date by [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/expanding-access-to-ai-with-chatgpt-ads/) (31 Aug 2026)
- [REPORTED] Pricing figures are US/vendor data, not OpenAI-published for India: ~$60 CPM at Feb pilot, reportedly down to ~$25 by April; CPC suggested max bids of $3-5; one India agency (upGrowth, a seller of these campaigns) cites avg CPM Rs 581, CPC Rs 13.67. Treat as anecdotal. — [WebSearch summary citing upGrowth and others](https://upgrowth.in/blog/chatgpt-ads-guide-india) (undated)
- [REPORTED] "OpenAI hasn't published a price, cost per thousand impressions or conversion rate for sponsored agents." — [Business of GTM](https://businessofgtm.com/articles/openai-chatgpt-business-profiles-agent-ads) (12 Nov 2026)
- [REPORTED] Shomair: "Think of the business agent as the organic experience. The sponsored agent is the paid experience, but the agent that sits behind it should be the same." Early sponsored tests showed "promising results." — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/openais-new-business-agents-give-brands-a-free-way-into-the-conversation-its-also-selling-as-an-ad/) (10 Nov 2026)

### Inferences
- The free agent is a funnel into the paid sponsored-agent format; the business model is the ad, not the profile.
- An Indian advertiser can buy ChatGPT Ads now, but sponsored agents may not yet be enabled for Indian accounts; verify in Ads Manager.

### Gaps
- No official pricing for sponsored agents; no India-specific CPM from OpenAI.
- Whether sponsored agents are enabled outside the US.
- Exact "Reach more people" promotion mechanics in Business Hub beyond one mention ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/businesses-gain-chatgpt-click-and-mention-counts-in-openais-business-hub/), 11 Nov 2026).

## 4. Verification, how ChatGPT chooses which businesses to show, analytics

### Takeaway
Verification is a claim step (phone, business email, domain or Shopify) plus Persona identity check; the blue check appears only after Persona approval. OpenAI says claiming and paying do not increase how often ChatGPT shows a business. Analytics are aggregate only (28 days): mentions, website clicks, discovery topics.

### Cited Findings
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] Claim via methods "such as phone, business email, website domain, or Shopify"; OpenAI emails after reviewing. Persona identity verification is needed before publishing changes, connecting a feed, viewing insights or creating an agent. Claiming alone gives no badge; the blue check appears after Persona approval. — [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] "Claiming does not guarantee higher placement or sales." ChatGPT keeps making independent recommendations; "Advertising doesn't influence ChatGPT's independent answers." ChatGPT may show a business without a claim or agent. — [OpenAI Businesses in ChatGPT page](https://openai.com/business/businesses-in-chatgpt/); [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] Insights show aggregate activity over past 28 days: Mentions (online business: ChatGPT references the business's website; brand name alone does not count; local business: ChatGPT shows a result for that location), Website clicks, Discovery topics (themes, not individual prompts or intent). Blank metric can mean below threshold. No individual prompts or chats shared. — [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt); [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)
- [REPORTED] Website clicks are clicks from supported ChatGPT surfaces, not completed visits or purchases; low counts may be withheld; "Share of voice isn't part of this beta." — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/openais-new-business-agents-give-brands-a-free-way-into-the-conversation-its-also-selling-as-an-ad/) (10 Nov 2026)
- [REPORTED] Shomair: claiming "should not have an impact on how frequently it appears," but clearer data could help ChatGPT surface a business in more relevant conversations. — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/openais-new-business-agents-give-brands-a-free-way-into-the-conversation-its-also-selling-as-an-ad/)
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] OpenAI does not verify every detail or resolve every factual dispute in submitted profiles. — [OpenAI policy](https://openai.com/policies/businesses-in-chatgpt/)
- [REPORTED] OpenAI has not published how ChatGPT ranks/selects businesses; no source I found describes the selection algorithm. (Absence of evidence.)

### Inferences
- Selection appears to be driven by ChatGPT's independent retrieval (web, feeds, other sources); the profile is a data input, not a ranking lever per OpenAI.

### Gaps
- No documentation of ranking/selection logic; reporting thresholds unpublished.

## 5. Limitations, criticisms, unknowns

### Takeaway
Key limits: beta, US-only agents, no transactions, no transcripts, data thresholds, OpenAI controls the agent, and trust/disclosure concerns about a seller bot inside a trusted assistant.

### Cited Findings
- [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL] Beta; US-only agents; no purchases/bookings/order lookup; Hub and Ads Manager agents don't sync; local businesses US-only; updates reviewed by OpenAI and can lag; ChatGPT may use other sources. — sources as above.
- [REPORTED] Real AI Dynamics critique: a seller's bot should say plainly it is a salesperson and cannot give a neutral comparison; the label is easy to miss on mobile; paid and free agents look the same; the "ads don't influence answers" pledge covers ChatGPT's replies, not the Business Agent. — [Real AI Dynamics](https://realaidynamics.com/chatgpts-new-business-agents-should-admit-they-are-salespeople/) (11 Nov 2026; opinion)
- [REPORTED] The Register on sponsored agents: "pretty much exactly like the chatbots you see on company websites, only run by OpenAI." — [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/09/16/openais-new-sponsored-agents-are-happy-to-chat-about-selling-you-things/5296946) (16 Sep 2026; opinion)
- [REPORTED] Analyst view: OpenAI sharing mentions data may erode third-party AEO/GEO tools, though OpenAI "isn't going to share everything" and ChatGPT is one of several platforms (Debra Aho Williamson, Sonata Insights). An agency CEO (Farhad Divecha, ACCURACAST) wants to compare OpenAI data against third-party estimates. — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/openais-new-business-agents-give-brands-a-free-way-into-the-conversation-its-also-selling-as-an-ad/) (10 Nov 2026)
- [REPORTED] Accuracy risk: feed/website price mismatch means the agent may quote wrong prices; businesses own that risk. — [Ecommerce Paradise](https://ecommerceparadise.com/openai-chatgpt-business-profiles-agents/) (10 Nov 2026; opinion). Policy confirms "Businesses are responsible for the information and instructions they provide" ([OpenAI policy](https://openai.com/policies/business-agents-policy/)).
- [REPORTED] Commercial pressure context: OpenAI's Shomair says OpenAI "need[s] to build an ad business"; priorities stated as user trust, user value, then advertiser value. — [AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/ai/in-openais-ad-business-advertisers-come-third/) (10 Nov 2026)
- Possible confusion: some press (e.g. windows/tech blogs) describe launch in generic terms; Google also launched a similar "Business Agent" in YouTube ads beta on 16 Sep in the US — [The Keyword (third-party site, not Google)](https://www.thekeyword.co/news/chatgpt-business-hub-agents) (11 Nov 2026, [REPORTED]; not verified against Google).

### Inferences
- The main strategic risk to businesses is dependence on a platform that controls the agent, data and ranking without offering share-of-voice, transcripts or purchase completion.
- For a comparison with Google Business Profile: GBP-style local claiming in ChatGPT is US-listings-only, whereas Google's is global; this comparison needs a separate Google source (not covered here).

### Gaps
- No independent measurements of performance or conversion; beta is days old.
- No official India statement for Business Hub, agents, or sponsored agents.
- Primary pages not fetched directly (network block); official text relied on Exa extracts and may be truncated.
