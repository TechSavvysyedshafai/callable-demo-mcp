## 1. How big and how fast-growing is apparel retail in Bihar and in tier-2/3 India; which segments grow fastest?

### Takeaway
India's apparel market is roughly Rs 9.3 lakh crore (FY25 est.), growing ~7% a year, with organised retail (41%) growing 10-13%. Tier-2/3 cities are about 23% of apparel demand and are the main growth zone, led by value fashion and private labels. No reliable Bihar-only apparel market size was found.

### Cited Findings
- Apparel market Rs 9.30 lakh crore in FY25 (estimated), ~7% CAGR since FY18, projected Rs 16 lakh crore (~USD 176 bn) by FY30. Organised retail ~41%, unorganised ~59%; organised retail expected to grow 10-13%. [FETCHED] — [CareEdge Ratings, 14 Jan 2026](https://www.careratings.com/uploads/newsfiles/1768407809_Indian%20Apparel%20Retail%20Market%20to%20Touch%20Rs%2016%20Lakh%20Crore.pdf); same numbers reported in [FashionUnited India, 20 Jan 2026](https://fashionunited.in/news/business/indias-apparel-retail-market-to-reach-176-billion-dollars-by-fy30/2026012052868)
- Value fashion estimated Rs 3.5 lakh crore in FY24, expected to reach Rs 5.0 lakh crore by FY30 (~7% CAGR), growth "particularly in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities". [FETCHED] — [CareEdge, Jan 2026](https://www.careratings.com/uploads/newsfiles/1768407809_Indian%20Apparel%20Retail%20Market%20to%20Touch%20Rs%2016%20Lakh%20Crore.pdf)
- "Approximately 23% of total apparel demand is expected to come from" tier-2/3 cities, with significant demand in value segment; organised value retailers moved value apparel there from largely unorganised to "somewhat organised". [FETCHED] — [CareEdge, Jan 2026](https://www.careratings.com/uploads/newsfiles/1768407809_Indian%20Apparel%20Retail%20Market%20to%20Touch%20Rs%2016%20Lakh%20Crore.pdf)

## 2. Style and fit trends rising in 2025-2026 (India and smaller cities)

### Takeaway
Evidence is qualitative: relaxed/baggy/wide-leg and oversized silhouettes are widely described as mainstream, but no sales share by fit and no Google Trends data could be obtained. Everything below is trade/brand commentary, not measured data.

### Cited Findings
- Mass-market denim brands (Spykar, Flying Machine) moving into relaxed and wide-leg cuts after years of skinny dominance; Levi's/Denizen continue classic straight-leg; Mufti/Numero Uno carry tapered high-rise. No percentages given. [SNIPPET, trade commentary] — [Apparel Resources](https://apparelresources.com/fashion-news/trends/denim-doesnt-pick-side-autumn-season/)

## 3. Offline vs online share in tier-2/3 and trend

### Takeaway
Online is ~22% of organised apparel retail (aim ~25% by FY30), but organised is only 41% of the market, so online is a minority of total apparel. Online fashion is already heavily non-metro: 59% of online fashion GMV comes from tier-2+ cities.

### Cited Findings
- Organised apparel: e-commerce 22% of organised market (about Rs 5.5 lakh cr in the chart's FY25E series, vs organised B&M Rs 1.75 lakh cr and unorganised Rs 2.05 lakh cr in the same chart; these chart figures look inconsistent with the Rs 9.3 lakh cr total, so quote only the percentages). Expected ~25% by FY30. [FETCHED] — [CareEdge, Jan 2026](https://www.careratings.com/uploads/newsfiles/1768407809_Indian%20Apparel%20Retail%20Market%20to%20Touch%20Rs%2016%20Lakh%20Crore.pdf)
- 59% of online fashion GMV from T2+ cities (3 years to Aug 2025) vs 46% for all online retail; 85% of India's population lives in T2+ cities; fashion is every fourth rupee of online retail spend. [FETCHED] — [Redseer, 7 Nov 2025](https://redseer.com/articles/the-year-round-wardrobe-why-online-fashion-is-defying-the-season)
- CareEdge: brick-and-mortar continues to dominate premium purchases; e-commerce to grow faster than B&M. [FETCHED] — [CareEdge](https://www.careratings.com/uploads/newsfiles/1768407809_Indian%20Apparel%20Retail%20Market%20to%20Touch%20Rs%2016%20Lakh%20Crore.pdf)
- Quick commerce entering fashion: Myntra M-Now (30-minute delivery, ~10,000 styles, pilot began Bengaluru), Blinkit/Zepto/Instamart tie-ups with Decathlon, Puma, FabIndia, Blinkit 10-minute fashion returns. Dark-store reach is metro-centred; Patna coverage not verified. [SNIPPET] — [Exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/marketing-news/myntra-enters-quick-comm-race-with-30-min-deliveries-138962.html), [FashionNetwork](https://in.fashionnetwork.com/news/Truebrowns-makes-quick-commerce-debut-with-m-now-and-blinkit,1713835.html)
- Redseer reports "Online Retail Heads for USD 90 Bn+ in 2026" (19 Oct 2026) and a piece on quick and value commerce (12 Oct 2026); I only saw the titles. — [Redseer](https://redseer.com/articles/online-retail-heads-for-usd-90-bn-in-2026-its-fastest-growth-in-five-years/)

### Inferences
- With ~59% unorganised and ~22% of organised online, roughly 85%+ of national apparel spend is still offline (arithmetic from CareEdge's shares: 41% x 22% = ~9% online; this is my calculation, and the CareEdge chart's 22% may be defined differently, so treat as rough).
- Online mostly competes on price-sub-Rs 800 items and wide choice; local shops can compete on fit trial, instant availability and credit/relationship.

### Gaps
- No tier-2/3 offline vs online split for apparel specifically; no Bihar split; no measured quick-commerce impact on small apparel stores.

## 4. Price bands where tier-2/3 shoppers buy jeans/T-shirts

### Takeaway
No direct survey of jeans/T-shirt price bands in tier-2/3 was found. Indirect evidence: growth concentrated under Rs 800 ASP online; GST rule favours garments up to Rs 2,500.

### Cited Findings
- Fastest-growing online fashion cluster is below Rs 800 ASP. [SNIPPET] — [Redseer, Feb 2026](https://redseer.com/articles/india-fashion-why-growth-is-concentrating-at-the-bottom-of-the-asp-curve/)
- Post-GST change: apparel priced up to Rs 2,500 carries 5% GST (earlier 12% for Rs 1,000-2,500); above Rs 2,500 is 18% (earlier 12%). [FETCHED] — [CareEdge, Jan 2026](https://www.careratings.com/uploads/newsfiles/1768407809_Indian%20Apparel%20Retail%20Market%20to%20Touch%20Rs%2016%20Lakh%20Crore.pdf) (effective date not stated there; CAIT credits the Sept 2025 GST cuts for festive demand)
- Value retailers pitch "stylish, high-quality apparel at competitive prices" as alternatives to local boutiques (Zudio, Max, Yousta, StyleUp). [FETCHED] — [CareEdge](https://www.careratings.com/uploads/newsfiles/1768407809_Indian%20Apparel%20Retail%20Market%20to%20Touch%20Rs%2016%20Lakh%20Crore.pdf)

### Inferences
- A practical anchor is jeans/T-shirt price points set against value chains; exact Zudio/V-Mart jeans prices were not verified and should be checked in-store or on their sites.

## 5. Seasonality a Patna shop should plan around

### Takeaway
Festive (Dhanteras/Diwali/Chhath) and wedding seasons are the known peaks; online fashion nationally shows a flatter year-round curve. No Patna-specific monthly sales data, and nothing found on college-admission-season apparel demand.

### Cited Findings
- Online fashion seasonality is lower than overall online retail; festive peak exists but is less pronounced, and tier-2+ towns and metros show near-identical seasonality. [FETCHED] — [Redseer, 7 Nov 2025](https://redseer.com/articles/the-year-round-wardrobe-why-online-fashion-is-defying-the-season)
- CAIT: textiles and garments ranked second in Diwali-season spending with ~12% share. [SNIPPET] — [Apparel Resources](https://apparelresources.com/business-news/retail/diwali-witnesses-record-trade-rs-3-75-lakh-crore/)
- CAIT 2025: Chhath trade forecast ~Rs 12,000 cr nationally, later headline reports "over Rs 50,000 crore" (conflicting figures, possibly different scopes; clothing share not extracted). [SNIPPET] — [CAIT forecast](https://cait.in/trade-worth-inr-12000-crores-expected-during-the-four-day-chhath-puja-festival-significant-sales-recorded-for-clothing-fruits-flowers-vegetables-sarees-and-clay-stoves/), [IANS, 28 Oct 2025](https://ianslive.in/chhath-puja-festival-witnesses-trade-worth-over-rs-50000-crore-cait--20251028095717)
- Patna Dhanteras 2025: reports of strong optimism (paddy harvest) but a Boring Road jeweller described a dip; jewellery-focused, not apparel. [SNIPPET] — [Patna Press](https://patnapress.com/bihar-dhanteras-markets-2025/)
- Sector recovery noted from Oct/Nov 2024 on festive and wedding-season footfalls. [FETCHED] — [CareEdge](https://www.careratings.com/uploads/newsfiles/1768407809_Indian%20Apparel%20Retail%20Market%20to%20Touch%20Rs%2016%20Lakh%20Crore.pdf)

### Inferences
- Calendar to plan around (from the sources plus general knowledge, not verified for Bihar): Diwali/Dhanteras/Chhath in Oct-Nov (2026 dates fall within these weeks), wedding season Nov-Feb and again Apr-Jun, and possibly summer college/school admission in Jun-Aug. Chhath and wedding dates shift yearly with the Hindu calendar; check the panchang.
- Fashion is becoming less peak-driven, so continuous restocking beats one big festive buy.

### Gaps
- No Patna/Bihar apparel seasonality data, no Gandhi Maidan/Boring Road apparel sales, no wedding-season apparel numbers, and no evidence on college admission season demand.
