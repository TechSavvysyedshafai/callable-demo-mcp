# Funding options for a small Bihar (Patna) apparel retailer wanting to expand

Research date: 20 Oct 2026. Page dates are given where the page showed one. "Unverified" = I could not confirm it on an official page. Rates change often; every bank number below must be re-checked with the branch before relying on it.

## 1. What are the current maximum amounts, interest rates, collateral rules, tenures, fees and eligibility for each national option (MUDRA, CGTMSE, SIDBI, PM SVANidhi, bank cash credit/OD, bank MSME loans, TReDS, NBFC)?

### Takeaway
MUDRA goes up to Rs 20 lakh (Tarun Plus), CGTMSE lets banks lend up to Rs 10 crore without collateral and explicitly covers retail trade, and big public banks price MSME loans at roughly 8-11 percent versus 14-35 percent at NBFCs. PM SVANidhi is for street vendors only, so a shop is almost certainly not eligible. I could not verify any SIDBI-specific retail scheme, nor PNB or Union Bank product terms.

### Cited Findings

**MUDRA (PMMY)**
- Loan limit raised from Rs 10 lakh to Rs 20 lakh in Budget 2024-25 (announced 23 Jul 2024), effective 24 Oct 2024. CGFMU guarantee extends to the enhanced loans — [PIB, 29 Oct 2024](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2069170)
- Categories: Shishu up to Rs 50,000; Kishore above Rs 50,000 to Rs 5 lakh; Tarun above Rs 5 lakh to Rs 10 lakh; Tarun Plus above Rs 10 lakh to Rs 20 lakh — [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2069170)
- Tarun Plus is only for borrowers who have previously taken and successfully repaid a Tarun loan — [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2069170)
- Loans are collateral-free, cover manufacturing, trading and services, and are given by banks, RRBs, SFBs, NBFCs and MFIs — [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2069170)
- SBI prices "Mudra" and some other schemes at a flat 11.05 percent under its 2020 EBLR table (old, 1 Jan 2020 data, so likely stale) — [SBI EBLR PDF](https://sbi.bank.in/documents/26274/109521/EBLR+Interest+Rates+for+MSMEs+on+SBI+Website.pdf/40fedc1a-4abb-d54a-0952-54e3f1cb7c8e?t=1577454721104)
- mudra.org.in/offerings could not be fetched (crawl error). Mudra interest rate, tenure and fee caps are therefore unverified from the official site.

**CGTMSE credit guarantee (CGS-I)**
- Maximum guarantee ceiling is Rs 10 crore per borrower (raised from Rs 5 crore, effective Apr 2025); for RRBs the lender limit is Rs 2 crore; for SFBs, RRBs etc. up to Rs 200 lakh — [CGTMSE scheme document, updated 1 Apr 2025](https://www.cgtmse.in/Default/ViewFile/?id=1743176302611_CGTMSE%20-%20Scheme%20Document%20CGS%20I_updated%20as%20on%20Apr%201%202025.pdf&path=Page)
- Retail and wholesale trade are explicitly eligible, aligned with other activities on cover, ceiling and fee — [CGTMSE scheme document](https://www.cgtmse.in/Default/ViewFile/?id=1743176302611_CGTMSE%20-%20Scheme%20Document%20CGS%20I_updated%20as%20on%20Apr%201%202025.pdf&path=Page); [SBI CGTMSE features](https://sbi.bank.in/documents/26274/3200842/CGTMSE+CONSOLIDATED+FEATURES.pdf/a2280ee1-15dc-eb9d-2ef8-cb0b4a28200b?t=1754630937332)
- Cover (guarantees approved on/after 1 Apr 2025): 85% for micro enterprises up to Rs 5 lakh; 75% above that; women entrepreneurs up to Rs 10 crore: 90%; SC/ST, PwD, aspirational districts, ZED-certified: 85%; all others 75%; extra 5% in RBI-identified credit-deficient districts — [SBI CGTMSE features](https://sbi.bank.in/documents/26274/3200842/CGTMSE+CONSOLIDATED+FEATURES.pdf/a2280ee1-15dc-eb9d-2ef8-cb0b4a28200b?t=1754630937332); [Maharashtra MSME summary of the same](https://msme.maharashtra.gov.in/scheme/credit-guarantee-scheme-for-micro-and-small-enterprises-cgs-i/)
- Annual guarantee fee (from 1 Apr 2025, same for trading): 0.37% up to Rs 10 lakh; 0.55% above 10-50 lakh; 0.60% above 50 lakh-1 crore; 0.85% above 1-2 crore; 1.00% above 2-5 crore; 1.10% above 5-8 crore; 1.20% above 8-10 crore. Charged on guaranteed amount in year 1, on outstanding amount afterwards. 10% concession for women/SC/ST/PwD, aspirational/credit-deficient districts, ZED-certified — [CGTMSE fee page](https://www.cgtmse.in/Home/VS/47); [scheme document](https://cgtmse.in/Default/ViewFile/?id=1743176302611_CGTMSE+-+Scheme+Document+CGS+I_updated+as+on+Apr+1+2025.pdf&path=Page)
- The lender decides whether the borrower or the bank pays the fee — [CGTMSE fee page](https://www.cgtmse.in/Home/VS/47). Bajaj Finance lists CGTMSE fee "up to 0.885% incl. taxes" in year 1 — [Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/what-are-the-interest-rate-on-msme-loan)
- Guarantee lasts the term-loan tenure, or 5-year renewable blocks for working capital alone — [scheme document](https://www.cgtmse.in/Default/ViewFile/?id=1743176302611_CGTMSE%20-%20Scheme%20Document%20CGS%20I_updated%20as%20on%20Apr%201%202025.pdf&path=Page)
- Guarantee cannot be taken if the account was restructured or in SMA-2 in the last year (relevant to a business with falling sales) — [SBI CGTMSE features](https://sbi.bank.in/documents/26274/3200842/CGTMSE+CONSOLIDATED+FEATURES.pdf/a2280ee1-15dc-eb9d-2ef8-cb0b4a28200b?t=1754630937332)
- A parliamentary committee (PIB, Mar 2026) found banks in practice still ask for collateral, over-rely on CIBIL scores, and guarantee fees plus bank charges inflate effective cost; 1.35 crore guarantees worth Rs 12.39 lakh crore issued up to 31 Dec 2025 — [PIB, Mar 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2238242&lang=1&reg=3)
- Reported (secondary source, not verified on RBI site): RBI notification of 9 Feb 2026 bars collateral on MSE loans up to Rs 20 lakh sanctioned/renewed from 1 Apr 2026; no prepayment charge on floating-rate MSE loans from 1 Jan 2026; decision within 14 working days for MSE loans up to Rs 25 lakh — [Capnix, checked 2 Oct 2026](https://capnix.ai/rates/msme-loan-interest-rate)

**PM SVANidhi**
- For street vendors holding a Certificate of Vending / ID card / Letter of Recommendation from the urban local body (or BDO in census towns). Loans: Rs 15,000 (12 months), Rs 25,000 (18 months), Rs 50,000 (36 months); 7% interest subsidy; lending period extended to 31 Mar 2030 — [MoHUA guidelines](https://www.mohua.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/01/bde957bc3a87fb001c05ddd09484edda.pdf); [PIB Cabinet release, Aug 2025](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2161157)
- Bank interest example: SBI EBLR 8.15% + 1.75% = 9.90% (from 1 Oct 2025); IOB quotes RLLR + 1.30% — [SBI](https://sbi.bank.in/web/get-business-product-information/pm-svanidhi); [IOB](https://www.iob.bank.in/en/restructured-pm-svanidhi-scheme)
- Not relevant to a fixed-premises shop in Patna that wants Rs 10 lakh+; the maximum is Rs 50,000.

**SBI (public-sector bank) MSME products, pages dated Oct 2026**
- SBI EBLR was raised to 8.15% + CRP + BSP, and RLLR to 7.75% + CRP, effective 15 Oct 2026 (page updated 14 Oct 2026) — [SBI EBLR page](https://sbi.bank.in/web/interest-rates/external-benchmark-based-lending-rate)
- SME Laghu Udyami: for micro enterprises and small retail traders, up to Rs 10 lakh, cash credit and/or term loan; margin nil up to Rs 50,000, 10% above that; working capital assessed at 20% of last-12-month account turnover; term loan up to 5 years (<Rs 5 lakh) or 7 years (Rs 5 lakh+); cash credit validity 3 years; processing fee Rs 1,000 + GST; "nil collateral", covered by CGFMU as under Mudra; needs satisfactory 2-year track record and 60% minimum score — [SBI SME Laghu Udyami page, updated 13 Oct 2026](https://sbi.bank.in/web/business/sme/sme-loans/sme-credit-card)
- SME Smart Score: MSME units including proprietorship, partnership, private limited, trading sector; Rs 10 lakh to Rs 50 lakh; margin 20% on working capital, 33% on term loan; term loan/dropline OD up to 7 years; cash credit renewed every 2 years; processing fee 0.40%; needs Udyam number, PAN, GST, satisfactory CIBIL/CMR — [SBI Smart Score page (page dated Feb 2024)](https://sbi.bank.in/web/business/sme/sme-loans/sme-smart-score)
- SME Digi Sugam: dropline OD Rs 1 lakh to Rs 50 lakh, for proprietorships with 2+ years of business and 2 years of GST and ITR; limit is 25% of last-12-month turnover (lower of GST sales, bank credits, projection); repayable over 36 months; flat unified charge Rs 10,000; CIBIL 650+ or CMR 1-7; top-up needs bank credits above 80% of GST sales and no SMA-1/2 in last 12 months — [SBI Digi Sugam (page dated Jul 2025)](https://sbi.bank.in/web/business/sme-digi-sugam)
- Contactless lending platform: cash credit and term loan up to Rs 5 crore, margin up to 20%, hypothecation of stock and receivables for cash credit, CGTMSE/CGFMU cover or collateral, guarantee fee borne by borrower — [SBI](https://sbi.bank.in/web/business/sme/sme-loans/loans-under-contactless-lending-platform)
- SBI processing fee schedule (from 1 Jun 2025): nil up to Rs 50,000; Rs 1,000 up to Rs 5 lakh; Rs 2,000 above 5-10 lakh; 0.40% above 10-50 lakh; the page says micro and small enterprise working-capital limits up to Rs 5 lakh are nil (the table text is garbled; confirm at branch) — [SBI advances charges PDF](https://sbi.bank.in/documents/16012/22770835/02052025_Advances+Related+Service+Charges+w.e.f.+01.06.2025.pdf/8af187b9-c59e-656a-68f4-061ce0af89fd?t=1746184789453)
- The "all-in" SBI MSME pricing is not stated on these pages (they say "linked to EBLR/EBR, based on rating"). SBI's EBR is stated as "Repo + 2.65%" on product pages — [SBI Laghu Udyami](https://sbi.bank.in/web/business/sme/sme-loans/sme-credit-card)

**Bank of Baroda, HDFC and other banks (secondary source, rates as published and checked 2 Oct 2026)**
- Bank of Baroda: 8.15% for loans up to Rs 50,000 to 10.50% for Rs 10-25 lakh (micro); for loans above Rs 25 lakh to Rs 7.5 crore, priced by CIBIL MSME Rank: CMR 1 at 8.20-9.65%, CMR 6 at 10.35-15.60% (figures are the aggregator's worked numbers from BoB's formula, not read from BoB's page by me) — [Capnix](https://capnix.ai/rates)
- HDFC Bank business loan 10.75-22.50%; Bandhan secured 11.69-15.18%, unsecured 13.00-25.91%; HDFC and Kotak reportedly charge no processing fee up to Rs 5 lakh to micro/small enterprises that supply Udyam — [Capnix](https://capnix.ai/rates/msme-loan-interest-rate)
- Union Bank, PNB: no pages fetched; terms UNVERIFIED.

**Cash credit / overdraft against stock and receivables**
- SBI cash credit is secured by hypothecation of all stock and receivables, repayable on demand, renewed yearly — [SBI contactless lending page](https://sbi.bank.in/web/business/sme/sme-loans/loans-under-contactless-lending-platform)
- Drawing power is assessed on turnover (20% of turnover for Laghu Udyami; 25% for Digi Sugam) with 10-25% margin depending on product — see SBI links above.
- Stock insurance is to be insisted on — [SBI Digi Sugam](https://sbi.bank.in/web/business/sme-digi-sugam)

**Invoice discounting / TReDS**
- TReDS is an RBI-regulated platform operating since 2017 where MSME sellers get paid early against invoices from corporates, government departments and PSUs; volume rose from Rs 40,000 crore (FY22) to Rs 3.5 lakh crore (FY26) — [PIB, 25 Sep 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2314945&lang=2&reg=48)
- CGTMSE cover on TReDS invoice discounting is live on M1xchange, RXIL and DTX (KredX): 75% of default amount, max Rs 10 crore exposure for an MSE buyer, Rs 2 crore for an MSE seller; both buyer and seller must be micro or small — [PIB, 25 Sep 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2314945&lang=2&reg=48)
- Budget 2026-27 (1 Feb 2026): TReDS mandated for CPSE purchases from MSMEs, GeM-TReDS link, TReDS receivables as asset-backed securities — [PIB, 1 Feb 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2221434)
- TReDS discount rates and platform fees: not found (UNVERIFIED). Relevance to a retail shop: low, because TReDS needs corporate/government buyers; a retailer selling to walk-in consumers has few such invoices. It could matter only for a stitching unit supplying uniforms or bulk orders to a company.

**NBFC / fintech small-business loans (published rates, 2026)**
- Lendingkart: interest 13.5-35% (schedule of charges) or "from 17.25%" (other pages); processing fee max 3% (schedule) / up to 4% (FAQ); tenure up to 3 years; loan Rs 1-50 lakh; penal charge 36% p.a. plus Rs 300 per missed instalment; no foreclosure charge on business loans — [Lendingkart schedule of charges](https://www.lendingkart.com/legal-compliance/schedule-of-charges/); [Lendingkart rates page](https://www.lendingkart.com/msme-loan/interest-rate/)
- Bajaj Finance: 14-23% (page text also shows 14-18% in one table), processing fee up to 4.72% incl. taxes, prepayment up to 4.72%, tenure up to 8 years (96 months), bounce charge Rs 1,500 — [Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/what-are-the-interest-rate-on-msme-loan)
- Other published starting points (Mar 2026): FlexiLoans from 12%, Tata Capital from 12%, Muthoot Fincorp 15-36%, IIFL up to 28% — [Paisabazaar, 5 Mar 2026](https://www.paisabazaar.com/bajaj-finserv/business-loan/); [Capnix](https://capnix.ai/rates)
- Lendingkart eligibility: business older than 12 months (6 months on one page), turnover at least Rs 75,000-90,000 in the last 3 months — [Lendingkart](https://www.lendingkart.com/msme-loan/interest-rate/); [Paisabazaar](https://www.paisabazaar.com/business-loan/lendingkart-finance/)

**SIDBI**
- Only SIDBI link found: SIDBI co-owns CGTMSE, and SIDBI plus Bihar created a Rs 150 crore fund-of-funds for scale-up start-ups (from a Bihar start-up presentation on SlideShare; low-quality source) — [CGTMSE home](https://www.cgtmse.in/); [SlideShare Bihar startup deck](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/startup-bihar-presentation_bbc-2024-pptx/277283040)
- No SIDBI direct-lending scheme terms were verified. Treat as UNVERIFIED.

**Eligibility by legal form**
- CGTMSE covers new and existing micro and small enterprises, including trading, with no distinction by form; sole proprietor, partners, or promoter-directors give personal guarantees but not third-party guarantees — [CGTMSE scheme document](https://www.cgtmse.in/Default/ViewFile/?id=1743176302611_CGTMSE%20-%20Scheme%20Document%20CGS%20I_updated%20as%20on%20Apr%201%202025.pdf&path=Page)
- SBI Smart Score accepts proprietorship, partnership, closely-held public and private limited companies; Digi Sugam is proprietorships/individuals only — SBI links above.
- Micro enterprise limits since 2025 (as cited by Bajaj): investment up to Rs 2.5 crore and turnover up to Rs 10 crore; small enterprise: investment up to Rs 25 crore and turnover up to Rs 100 crore — [Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/what-are-the-interest-rate-on-msme-loan)

### Inferences
- A one-shop retailer fits SBI Laghu Udyami (up to Rs 10 lakh, nil collateral, Rs 1,000 fee) first, then Smart Score or a CGTMSE-covered loan up to Rs 50 lakh, with MUDRA Kishore/Tarun as the parallel route. For loans in the Rs 5-10 lakh band, the SBI limit logic (20% of turnover) means annual turnover under about Rs 50 lakh will cap the working-capital limit well below Rs 10 lakh.
- Because CGTMSE fee is only 0.37-0.55% per year, the extra cost of a guaranteed bank loan over an unguaranteed one is small; NBFC loans cost roughly 4-20 percentage points a year more in interest plus 2-5% in upfront fees.
- PM SVANidhi should be dropped from the shortlist.

## 2. Which Bihar state schemes give capital or interest subsidy for retail/trading versus manufacturing, and are retail shops eligible?

### Takeaway
Bihar's flagship small-entrepreneur scheme (Mukhyamantri Udyami Yojana) funds only a listed menu of manufacturing and service projects, not a pure retail shop, but it does list readymade-garment and hosiery units, so a small stitching unit could qualify. Bihar's big investment package (BIIPP 2025) targets projects above Rs 50 lakh in manufacturing and is not a retail scheme. Several eligibility details conflict between sources and must be checked on udyami.bihar.gov.in.

### Cited Findings

**Mukhyamantri Udyami Yojana (MMUY) - includes the Mahila (women), Yuva, SC/ST, EBC, Minority components**
- Per the department's own About page: each unit gets up to Rs 10 lakh total; 50% (max Rs 5 lakh) as subsidy/grant and 50% (max Rs 5 lakh) as an interest-free loan; only the Mukhyamantri Yuva Udyami component charges 1% annual interest; Rs 25,000 per unit is spent on training and monitoring; implemented by Bihar Start-up Fund Trust — [Udyami Bihar About Us](https://udyami.bihar.gov.in/about-us)
- Loan repayment is 84 instalments (7 years) — [Goodreturns, 27 Feb 2026](https://www.goodreturns.in/classroom/bihar-mukhyamantri-udyami-yojana-2026-how-to-register-for-the-scheme-step-by-step-guide-1492653.html); [Moneycontrol, 27 Feb 2026](https://www.moneycontrol.com/education/bihar-mukhyamantri-udyami-yojana-2026-registration-opens-for-up-to-rs-10-lakh-business-assistance-check-eligibility-interest-rates-and-selection-process-article-13845537.html)
- FY 2025-26 round: portal open 25 Feb to 15 Mar 2026, extended to 23 Mar 2026; lottery on 20 May 2026; document upload deadline 24 Jun 2026 — [Udyami Bihar portal notices](https://udyami.bihar.gov.in/)
- FY 2025-26 targets: 2,000 selections per component for four components (8,000), 1,247 for Minority, 100 for Divyang, total 9,347 — [FY2025-26 guideline copy hosted on biharjobalert.com](https://biharjobalert.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Bihar-udyami-Yojana-10-lakh-2026.pdf) (third-party copy of the department notice)
- Eligibility (same document): permanent Bihar resident; at least class 10+2 / ITI / polytechnic diploma; age 18-50; unit may be proprietorship, partnership, LLP or Pvt Ltd; proprietorship must be on the entrepreneur's personal PAN. Mahila component: women of all categories. Yuva component: men from General and BC-02 only — [guideline copy](https://biharjobalert.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Bihar-udyami-Yojana-10-lakh-2026.pdf)
- Applicants must choose from the department's approved project list (the FAQ says 58 listed projects in an older version) — [Udyami FAQ PDF (garbled font)](https://udyami.bihar.gov.in/pdf/FAQ.pdf); [guideline copy](https://biharjobalert.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Bihar-udyami-Yojana-10-lakh-2026.pdf)
- The current project list is manufacturing- and service-based (food processing, furniture, repair shops, salon, cyber cafe, etc.). I found no general retail/trading shop on the list. Apparel-related entries on the list: Readymade garments (shirt, frock, pyjama, kurta, kurti, nightie), Readymade garments hosiery/knitting (T-shirt, leggings, track suit, etc.), embroidery/zardozi, fabric dyeing, printing and finishing, bed sheet/pillow cover sets, powerloom — [Bihar udyamiuser project list](https://udyamiuser.bihar.gov.in/project-list); [Udyami MMUY page](https://udyami.bihar.gov.in/mmuy)
- A model DPR for Readymade Garments exists (machine list starting with lock-stitch machines) — [model DPR](https://udyami.bihar.gov.in/pdf/model-dpr/37.%20Readymade%20Garments.pdf)
- Exclusions in an older (2022) ClearTax summary: government/contract staff earning above Rs 15,000/month, people already using PMEGP, Mudra, SIPB or start-up schemes of the Industries Department, elected representatives, ration/kerosene dealers, unit outside own district — [ClearTax, 2022](https://cleartax.in/s/bihar-mukhyamantri-udyami-yojana). This may be outdated; confirm in the 2025-26 guideline.
- CONFLICT: two aggregator sites say the scheme is only for a new enterprise, not an existing business, and one claims General category is not eligible — [Schemes in India, May 2026](https://schemesinindia.in/schemes/bihar/mukhyamantri-udyami-yojana-bihar); [SkillUPBihar, Apr 2026](https://www.skillupbihar.in/schemes/mukhyamantri-udyami-yojana). The guideline copy above lists a General-category Yuva component, so the SkillUPBihar claim looks wrong. Whether an existing shop owner can use the scheme for a separate new stitching unit is UNVERIFIED.
- The scheme's stated purpose is to remove collateral and margin-money problems for bank loans — [About Us](https://udyami.bihar.gov.in/about-us)

**Bihar Industrial Investment Promotion Package 2025 (BIIPP 2025)**
- Minimum project cost above Rs 50 lakh; new units must apply on the Single Window portal by 31 Mar 2026; milestones by 31 Mar 2027; incentives are options (choose one): interest subvention (10% or actual rate, 12% for micro and small, for 5 years, capped at 15-50% of approved project cost depending on sector priority) plus SGST reimbursement; or special SGST package (large/mega only); or capital subsidy 20-30% for large projects — [BookMyCA summary](https://www.bookmyca.in/subsidy/state-schemes/bihar/industrial-investment-promotion-package-2025)
- Micro and small units also get CGTMSE fee reimbursement under option 1; 15% extra interest-subvention for SC, ST, EBC, women etc. (per same summary) — [BookMyCA](https://www.bookmyca.in/subsidy/state-schemes/bihar/industrial-investment-promotion-package-2025)
- Region B (standard region) includes Patna; Region A (focus) includes most other districts — [BookMyCA](https://www.bookmyca.in/subsidy/state-schemes/bihar/industrial-investment-promotion-package-2025)
- Textile and leather: "high priority" only with at least 50 direct employees; otherwise a garment unit would fall in a lower category — [BookMyCA](https://www.bookmyca.in/subsidy/state-schemes/bihar/industrial-investment-promotion-package-2025)
- Employment incentive: up to Rs 2,000 per employee per month for ESI/EPF for 5 years (textile units use a 2022 textile policy); quality certification 50% up to Rs 2 lakh — [BookMyCA](https://www.bookmyca.in/subsidy/state-schemes/bihar/industrial-investment-promotion-package-2025)
- The package covers units in the "industrial" sense (manufacturing, with a negative list); I found no retail or trading shop eligibility.
- CONFLICT on validity: package "till 31 March 2026" per several sources; one aggregator says 30 Jun 2026; the Bihar SWC site lists a "Notification Extension of BIIPP 2025" that I did not open — [SWC Bihar policies page](https://swc2.bihar.gov.in/investor/policies-acts); [IndustrialIncentives.com](https://industrialincentives.com/schemes/bihar-industrial-investment-promotion-package-biipp-2025). Status as of Oct 2026 is UNVERIFIED.

**Bihar Start-up Policy 2022**
- Valid 5 years from notification; eligible entity: Pvt Ltd, registered partnership or LLP, up to 10 years old, turnover never above Rs 100 crore, working on innovation or a scalable high-employment model, registered and taxed in Bihar — [Policy PDF (Invest India)](https://static.investindia.gov.in/s3fs-public/2022-10/StartUp-Policy-English-2022.pdf)
- Seed funding up to Rs 10 lakh as a 10-year interest-free loan (tranches Rs 4 lakh then Rs 6 lakh); matching loan equal to funds raised from angels or Cat-I AIFs up to Rs 50 lakh at 5% p.a. with 2-year moratorium and 7-year EMI; 2% success fee on angel money raised; up to Rs 3 lakh for accelerator programs; extra 5% for women/SC/ST (policy) and 15% for differently abled (policy text says SC/ST 15%; Startup India page says SC/ST 5%, so conflict) — [Bihar policy snapshot (May 2026)](https://betastate.bihar.gov.in/file_2/FileUpload/2026/May/08-May-2026/40/Publication/9b53bf71-7a12-469d-b1fb-73372538aa4d.pdf); [Startup India Bihar page](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/state-startup-policies/Bihar-state-policy.html)
- Focus areas listed include "Fashion / Apparels / Handicrafts" and "E-Commerce" — [Sabagris summary of Startup Cell GoB](https://sabagris.com/programme-startup-cell--gob) (secondary source)
- A conventional retail shop would not normally qualify as a start-up (needs innovation or scalable model). A distinct online own-label apparel venture set up as a Pvt Ltd/LLP might, if certified; this is untested.

**Other schemes**
- PMEGP (central): readymade garment business is not eligible for PMEGP per an old FAQ (loan margin money only to first-generation entrepreneurs); trading sales outlets only in limited cases (Khadi/village-industry products, outlets backed by manufacturing); max Rs 20 lakh for trade/service projects; all units must have Udyam registration — [PMEGP projects page](https://common-pmegp.msme.gov.in/Home/ProjectDetailList); [Banking School FAQ (unofficial, old)](https://bankingschool.co.in/loans-and-advances/pmegp-frequently-asked-questions/). ## 3. What are realistic total costs and risks for a retailer with falling sales, from the lender's viewpoint?

### Takeaway
Banks assess limits as a percentage of turnover and demand credits matching GST sales, so falling turnover directly shrinks the limit and can block CGTMSE cover. NBFC money is easy to get but costs 14-35 percent plus 2-5 percent upfront and has a 36 percent penal rate.

### Cited Findings
- SBI sizes limits as 20% (Laghu Udyami) or 25% (Digi Sugam) of turnover; Digi Sugam uses the lowest of GST sales, bank credits or next-year projection, and top-ups need bank credits above 80% of GST sales and no SMA-1/2 in the last year — [SBI Laghu Udyami](https://sbi.bank.in/web/business/sme/sme-loans/sme-credit-card); [SBI Digi Sugam](https://sbi.bank.in/web/business/sme-digi-sugam)
- Banks ask for satisfactory track record over 2 years and CIBIL / CMR checks (Digi Sugam: CMR 1-7 or score 650+; no write-off or settlement in 2 years) — [SBI Digi Sugam](https://sbi.bank.in/web/business/sme-digi-sugam)
- CGTMSE cover cannot be added to an account that was restructured or SMA-2 in the past year — [SBI CGTMSE features](https://sbi.bank.in/documents/26274/3200842/CGTMSE+CONSOLIDATED+FEATURES.pdf/a2280ee1-15dc-eb9d-2ef8-cb0b4a28200b?t=1754630937332)
- Pricing rises with weak credit grade: BoB CMR 6 pays up to 15.60% versus 8.20-9.65% for CMR 1 (secondary) — [Capnix](https://capnix.ai/rates)
- Cost stack for NBFC: interest 13.5-35%, processing fee up to 3-4.72%, documentation about Rs 1,200, NACH Rs 30, GST on fees, penal 36% p.a. — [Lendingkart schedule](https://www.lendingkart.com/legal-compliance/schedule-of-charges/); [Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/what-are-the-interest-rate-on-msme-loan)
- Committee noted effective borrowing cost is inflated by guarantee fees, bank charges and insurance premiums, and that banks still ask for collateral in practice — [PIB, Mar 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2238242&lang=1&reg=3)
- Lendingkart minimum turnover test: Rs 75,000-90,000 in the last 3 months — [Lendingkart](https://www.lendingkart.com/msme-loan/interest-rate/)

### Inferences
- Illustrative cost (my calculation, not sourced): Rs 10 lakh over 5 years at about 10-11% costs roughly Rs 2.8-3.1 lakh in interest, plus Rs 1,000-2,000 fee and about 0.4% CGTMSE fee if passed on. The same Rs 10 lakh from an NBFC over 3 years at 18% with 3% fee costs about Rs 3.0 lakh interest plus Rs 30,000+ fees, but EMIs are about Rs 36,000 a month versus about Rs 21,000 for the bank loan over 5 years.
- If turnover is falling, a lender will size a working-capital limit off the lowest of GST sales, bank credits and projections. A realistic limit may be 20-25% of current turnover, not what the owner needs for growth. Declining sales also raise the chance of SMA flags, which block CGTMSE and top-ups.
- Taking new debt to expand while sales fall is the main risk. The owner should show a plan with specific recovery drivers (own-label margin, online orders) and keep GST sales and bank credits aligned.

### Gaps
- No lender document explicitly describing how it treats declining turnover; the points above are inferred from limit formulas.
- No Bihar NPA or retail-default statistics found.

## 4. How do equity routes compare (angel/partner, SME IPO) on cost, time and eligibility?

### Takeaway
SME IPO is not realistic for a small shop: it needs a public limited company, 3 years of track record, at least Rs 1 crore operating profit in 2 of 3 years, and costs 8-14% of issue size. Angel or partner equity is cheaper in cash but dilutes control, and Bihar's start-up policy only rewards innovative, scalable start-ups.

### Cited Findings
- NSE Emerge: company under Companies Act, post-issue paid-up capital up to Rs 25 crore, 3-year track record, EBITDA Rs 1 crore from operations in 2 of the last 3 years, positive net worth, positive free cash flow to equity in 2 of 3 years (page updated 4 Aug 2026) — [NSE Emerge criteria](https://www.nseindia.com/static/companies-listing/raising-capital-public-issues-emerge-eligibility-criteria)
- BSE SME: net worth at least Rs 1 crore for 2 preceding years, net tangible assets Rs 3 crore, leverage within 3:1, 3-year track record — [BSE SME criteria](https://www.bsesme.com/static/getlisted/criteriaisting.aspx?expandable=0)
- Cost: total typically 8-14% of issue size; merchant banker 2-5%, legal Rs 15-50 lakh, audit/diligence Rs 10-40 lakh, registrar Rs 3-10 lakh, marketing Rs 10-25 lakh — [SME Advisory](https://www.smeadvisory.in/blog/sme-ipo-india-guide) (advisor blog; indicative). Another advisor says commonly up to about 12% — [Regitom, 12 Oct 2026](https://www.regitom.com/sme-ipo-india-eligibility-process-sebi-norms-2026)
- A SEBI working group (Oct 2026) proposes replacing the paid-up-capital test with market-cap bands and requiring average operating profit of at least Rs 3 crore over 3 years; this is a proposal, not final — [SEBI Working Group report, Oct 2026](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/commondocs/oct-2026/WG%20Report%20for%20Review%20of%20SME%20Provisions_p.pdf)
- Government equity-side support: Budget 2026-27 announced a Rs 10,000 crore SME Growth Fund for "champion" MSMEs and a Rs 2,000 crore top-up to the Self-Reliant India Fund for micro enterprises — [PIB, 1 Feb 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2221434). A parliamentary committee noted it needed operationalisation (Mar 2026) — [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2238242&lang=1&reg=3). Eligibility for a small retailer is not defined; UNVERIFIED.
- Bihar Start-up Policy: angel success fee 2%, matching loan up to Rs 50 lakh at 5% — [Bihar policy snapshot](https://betastate.bihar.gov.in/file_2/FileUpload/2026/May/08-May-2026/40/Publication/9b53bf71-7a12-469d-b1fb-73372538aa4d.pdf)

### Inferences
- A shop with falling sales and sub-Rs-1-crore profit cannot list. Timeline for an SME IPO is typically many months of preparation (my estimate; not sourced) and requires conversion to a public company.
- A friendly-partner or angel route is quicker but has no standard price; I found no data on angel terms for Patna retail. Debt cost 10-11% is a known number, equity cost depends on valuation.

### Gaps
- No data on typical angel/partner investment size, valuation or time for small retail in Bihar.
- No timeline source for SME IPO.
- SME Growth Fund eligibility and launch status.

## 5. What documents are usually needed, and what are the typical timelines?

### Takeaway
Udyam registration (mandatory for CGTMSE cover), PAN, KYC, 2 years of GST and ITR, bank statements and a stock statement are standard. Quick digital loans can be disbursed in about 3 days at NBFCs; RBI-linked norms say 14 working days for small MSE loans; Bihar's state scheme runs on a yearly cycle of about 5 months from application to document verification.

### Cited Findings
- Udyam Registration Certificate is mandatory to get a CGTMSE guarantee (SBI feature note section 5.1) and for SBI Smart Score, with online verification at udyamregistration.gov.in — [SBI CGTMSE features](https://sbi.bank.in/documents/26274/3200842/CGTMSE+CONSOLIDATED+FEATURES.pdf/a2280ee1-15dc-eb9d-2ef8-cb0b4a28200b?t=1754630937332); [SBI Smart Score](https://sbi.bank.in/web/business/sme/sme-loans/sme-smart-score)
- SBI Digi Sugam: KYC, Udyam, PAN, GSTN, at least 2 years of ITR (xml/json), GST data for 24+ months, account statement uploaded or fetched via account aggregator — [SBI Digi Sugam](https://sbi.bank.in/web/business/sme-digi-sugam)
- SBI Smart Score: PAN, Udyam, GST (if applicable), satisfactory commercial and consumer CIC reports — [SBI Smart Score](https://sbi.bank.in/web/business/sme/sme-loans/sme-smart-score)
- Lendingkart: business registration proof, bank statements, GST filings, KYC; funds within about 3 days — [Lendingkart](https://www.lendingkart.com/)
- Stock insurance is to be insisted on for stock-hypothecation limits — [SBI Digi Sugam](https://sbi.bank.in/web/business/sme-digi-sugam)
- Mukhyamantri Udyami documents: matric and intermediate certificates, caste certificate (if applicable), residence certificate, Aadhaar, PAN, bank statement with cancelled cheque, photo and signature; bank account (current account for proprietorship per ClearTax 2022) — [FAQ PDF](https://udyami.bihar.gov.in/pdf/FAQ.pdf); [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/bihar-mukhyamantri-udyami-yojana)
- Mukhyamantri Udyami timeline FY 2025-26: application window 25 Feb to 23 Mar 2026; lottery 20 May 2026; draft-applicant documents due 24 Jun 2026. Money is released in instalments, each needing a utilisation certificate within 90 days — [Udyami Bihar portal](https://udyami.bihar.gov.in/); [FAQ PDF (partly garbled)](https://udyami.bihar.gov.in/pdf/FAQ.pdf)
- Reported: banks must decide on MSE loans up to Rs 25 lakh within 14 working days (RBI Master Direction, via secondary source) — [Capnix](https://capnix.ai/rates/msme-loan-interest-rate)
- PM SVANidhi average processing time about 23 days (street vendors only) — [PIB FAQ, 30 May 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/FaqDetails.aspx?ModuleId=4&NoteId=158727&lang=1&reg=3)

### Inferences
- Plan on 2-6 weeks for a bank/CGTMSE loan, 3-10 days for an NBFC, and 4-7 months for Bihar's Udyami cycle if the window opens in Feb 2027 (my extrapolation from the 2025-26 calendar).
- A tidy file would hold: Udyam certificate, GST returns for 24 months, 2 years of ITR with computations, 12-24 months bank statements for the operating account, a stock statement and insurance, KYC and CIBIL report, quotations for any machines, a short business plan.

### Gaps
- Bank-wise official timelines (SBI, PNB, Union, BoB) not published on pages read.
- Whether SBI Laghu Udyami / Mudra need a stock statement at the sanction stage is not stated.
- Udyam registration process and fee (free, per my knowledge) not re-verified on udyamregistration.gov.in.
