## Where people shop in Patna, and local factors that hurt small independents

### Takeaway
Boring Road is the upmarket high-street (congested, high rents); Maurya Lok and Fraser Road/Dak Bungalow are older commercial hubs; Kankarbagh is a student/residential hub. Shop asking rents on Boring Road are about Rs 110-146 per sq ft per month (listing asks, undated). Patna-specific evidence on parking, online delivery and GST harm is thin.

### Cited Findings
- Boring Road is described as Patna's most upmarket shopping area; Maurya Lok as one of the oldest/major shopping districts. — [Wikipedia Boring Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boring_Road), [Wikipedia Maurya Lok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurya_Lok)
- Guide says Boring Road is "always congested", worst on weekend evenings, and that high rent pushes up prices even at non-branded shops (blog, undated). — [rahul.biz Patna markets](https://www.rahul.biz/bihar/best-markets-in-patna)
- Patna Press (search summary of a winter report) lists Boring Road, Fraser Road, Gandhi Maidan, Dak Bungalow, Kankarbagh, Bazar Samiti and Maurya Lok as commercial hubs with rising footfall; seasonal winter-wear fair at High Court shrine complex. Page not opened. — [Patna Press](https://patnapress.com/east-india-budget-winter-markets-woollens/)
- Boring Road shop asking rents: 500 sq ft ground-floor about Rs 55,000/month with Rs 5 lakh deposit (about Rs 110/sq ft); 400 sq ft at Rs 55,000 (about Rs 137/sq ft); 240 sq ft at Rs 35,000 (about Rs 146/sq ft). Listings, undated, asking not transacted. — [RealEstateIndia](https://www.realestateindia.com/patna-property/commercial-shops-for-rent-in-boring-road-patna.htm), [Squareyards](https://www.squareyards.com/rent/property-for-rent-in-kidwaipuri-patna)
- Orbit Mall Patna promotional post claims rental potential of Rs 1-7 lakh/month per full showroom (promoter marketing, unverified). — [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/mundeshwari/posts/invest-where-future-prosperity-awaits-orbit-mall-patnaexperience-the-perfect-ble/1485233786940808/)
- Emporium Mall: 2,200 sq ft 1st-floor space asked Rs 58,000/month (about Rs 26/sq ft) — promoter post, unverified. — [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/realgreenhomespvtltd/posts/a-prime-location-can-make-all-the-difference-to-your-business-emporium-mall-brin/1079576915003848/)
- GST on apparel since 22 Sep 2025: 5% up to Rs 2,500 per piece, 18% above (earlier: 5% up to Rs 1,000, 12% above). Several sites still show old rates. — [Franchise India](https://www.franchiseindia.com/index.php/insights/en/news/gst-relief-likely-for-apparel-footwear-under-2500-as-council-moves-to-expand-5-tax-slab.57362), [Accountune](https://accountune.com/gst-on-garments-above-2500)
- National: fashion quick commerce growing (Myntra M-Now 30-min, Ajio Rush 4-hr in six cities); analysts say clothing is a planned purchase and may suit it less; small-town impact unclear. — [Apparel Resources](https://apparelresources.com/business-news/retail/apparel-brands-hopping-10-minute-shopping-bandwagon/)

### Inferences
- At Rs 110-146/sq ft, a 400 sq ft Boring Road shop costs about Rs 45-58k/month before staff and power, so it needs roughly Rs 1.5-2 lakh+ monthly sales at a 30-35% margin to cover rent alone (my arithmetic, not a sourced figure).
- Under the 5% slab, jeans/tees under Rs 2,500 are lower-tax than before for mid-range goods (inference from rate change).

### Gaps
- E-way bill rules (commonly Rs 50,000 consignment threshold): NOT verified in this run.
- Patna parking, footfall shift from street to mall, local traders' complaints: not found.
- Online delivery penetration in Patna: not found.
