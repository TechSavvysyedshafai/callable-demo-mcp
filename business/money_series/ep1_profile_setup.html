Google Business Profile vs the new ChatGPT business profile

Google wins today. The ChatGPT profile is a free extra, not a replacement. Reasons, charts and what is still unknown are below.

Winner today

Free add-on

Chance a ChatGPT profile brings a typical Indian shop measurable customers within 6 months (my judgment)

  • OpenAI's Business Hub started on 10 Nov 2026. It is a beta, four days old. No results data exists.
  • Local shops and restaurants can claim only US listings. Business Agents are US-only. India is neither confirmed nor excluded for website-based profiles.
  • Most Indian small shops have no website (about 18% to 27%), and the ChatGPT claim starts with a website.
  • AI chatbots send about 0.3% to 1.1% of website traffic. Organic search sends about 43%.

Why ChatGPT rarely uses your Google profile

  • In a BrightLocal vendor study of 200,085 prompts (US, UK, Australia), the Google profile supplied 28.5% of citations across Google's AI answers. ChatGPT barely used it.
  • Yelp was a source in 80% of ChatGPT local answers. Yelp is thin in India, so directories such as Justdial probably matter more there (my judgment).
  • Repeating the same ChatGPT prompt gave an overlapping result only 23% of the time. A one-time listing cannot promise stable visibility.
  • ChatGPT's business details fully matched Google's in 55.6% of 10,000 US businesses, mostly wrong phone numbers.

What helps every platform: a real website, the same name, address, phone and hours everywhere, and good reviews.

If you sell this as a freelancer

  • Sell: Google profile setup, the same data on Google, Justdial and the website, a review routine, and a monthly check of what Google and ChatGPT say about the shop.
  • Price idea (my extrapolation, not an observed price): about ₹3,000 to ₹8,000 once, plus ₹1,500 to ₹4,000 a month.
  • Never promise "rank in ChatGPT" or "customers from ChatGPT". OpenAI says claiming does not change visibility and no outcome data exists.
  • The shop owner should own the Google and ChatGPT accounts, not you.

What is verified, and what is not

  • No outcome data exists for ChatGPT profiles. The beta is four days old.
  • India availability is unconfirmed for Business Hub, agents and sponsored agents. OpenAI lists no countries or dates.
  • Most numbers come from vendors or search summaries: the traffic shares, BrightLocal and SOCi studies, the 18.4% website sample and all rupee prices.
  • Official OpenAI and Google text was read through search extracts and may be incomplete.
  • The 200 million India figure is my arithmetic. The 1.2 billion figure is a company claim seen through secondary sources.
  • Not found anywhere: India-specific studies on ChatGPT local sources, Hindi accuracy, Google profile adoption by Indian shops, or suspension rates.
  • What would change my mind: OpenAI opening Indian local listings and the mentions and clicks counters showing real numbers.
