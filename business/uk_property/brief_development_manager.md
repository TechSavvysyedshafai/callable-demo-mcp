# Brief for development-management firms

Send to at least three firms. Fill the [brackets]. Keep a copy of every reply.

Subject: Enquiry - development management for a small first residential scheme, [area]

Hello [name],

We are forming a UK limited company to buy and build one small residential scheme in [London borough / area]. We are first-time developers and would like a firm to run the scheme day to day under a written development management agreement. Please tell us whether this is a size and type of job you take on, and on what terms.

**Who we are**
- A UK-resident director ([name], lives in [area]) who will sign documents and meet in person.
- A second shareholder who lives outside the UK and works remotely. The split is not final; we are taking tax and legal advice on the structure.
- No development track record yet. We will say so to every lender and adviser.

**The scheme (indicative, not yet bought)**
- Site: [address or area]. Planning status: [granted ref ____ / pre-application / none yet].
- Homes: [number and type, floor area].
- Budget before finance: about £[ ] (land £[ ], build £[ ], fees and contingency £[ ]).
- Expected sale value: about £[ ] (based on [evidence: sold prices of similar new homes]).
- Intended exit: sell on completion.
- Time: start by [date], sale within [months].

**Our rules (we will not proceed without these)**
1. Planned profit of at least 20% of sale value, and still break-even if the sale price comes in 15% lower.
2. Planning permission granted before purchase, or a purchase conditional on it.
3. A fixed-price building contract and a contingency of at least 10% of build cost.
4. No uncapped personal guarantees.
5. Our own solicitor, separate from the lender's and the seller's.

**What we are asking you to do**
- Check feasibility and costs before we buy, and tell us if the numbers do not work.
- Manage the planning conditions, design team, tender and the building contract.
- Report to us monthly: costs against budget, programme, risks.
- Check the contractor's valuations before stage payments, working with the lender's monitoring surveyor.
- Manage the sale.

**Please answer in writing**
1. Do you take on a scheme of this size for first-time clients? What is the smallest you do?
2. Your fee: fixed sum, a percentage of build cost or of sale value, or profit share? What is included and what is extra? Is any part paid before the scheme starts?
3. Three finished schemes of similar size, with the address, the original budget and the final cost, and the contact of the client. May we visit one?
4. Your professional memberships (for example RICS or CIOB), and your professional indemnity and public liability insurance (cover amount and insurer).
5. Who exactly will work on our scheme, and how many other schemes will they manage at the same time?
6. Do you work with clients who are partly based abroad? How will you report and take instructions?
7. Do you or a related company own land, build, or lend? Any other interest in what you recommend?
8. A draft development management agreement, and your standard terms for ending it.
9. What would make you advise us not to proceed?

**How we will compare replies**
Score each firm 1 to 5 on: fit and size, fee clarity, track record you can verify, insurance, conflicts, reporting, and how plainly they answer. We prefer a plain "no" to an optimistic "yes".

We have not committed to any site or firm. We will reply to all firms by [date].
