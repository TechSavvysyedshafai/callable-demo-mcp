# Brief for a development-finance broker

Send to two or three regulated brokers. Fill the [brackets]. Ask each to quote from the same lenders so you can compare.

Subject: Indicative terms for a small ground-up residential scheme, first-time developer

Hello [name],

We are planning one small ground-up residential scheme and would like indicative terms from several lenders. Nothing is bought yet. We would like to understand what is possible and at what cost before we commit to a site.

**Borrower**
- A new UK limited company, [name or "to be formed"].
- Director: one UK-resident individual (lives in [area], long UK credit history).
- Second shareholder: lives outside the UK. Ownership split to be agreed.
- No previous development experience. We will use a development management firm and a fixed-price contractor.

**The scheme (indicative)**
- Location: [borough / postcode area]. Planning: [granted ref ____ / not yet].
- Homes: [number, type, floor area].
- Costs before finance: about £[ ] (land £[ ], build £[ ], fees and contingency £[ ]).
- Expected sale value: about £[ ] (evidence: [sold prices]).
- Build period [ ] months, sale period [ ] months.
- Equity we can put in: £[ ] in cash.

**Please ask each lender and tell us, in writing**
1. Maximum loan as a share of cost and as a share of sale value.
2. Interest rate (monthly or yearly), whether it is rolled up, and whether it is fixed or variable.
3. All fees: arrangement, exit, broker, legal, valuation, monitoring surveyor visits, extension fees.
4. Cash we must put in, and when (before the first drawdown?).
5. **Personal guarantees:** is one required? Unlimited or capped? From whom? Are any lenders available with no personal guarantee, and at what price?
6. Treatment of a shareholder who lives abroad: identity checks, extra security, any restriction on residency or country.
7. How and when money is released (stages), who checks the work, and how long the lender takes to pay.
8. What happens if costs run over or the sale is delayed. What does the lender do, and what does it charge?
9. Minimum profit margin the lender requires, and any rule about planning being in place before drawdown.
10. Timetable from application to first drawdown.

**Our limits**
- We will not sign an uncapped personal guarantee.
- We want to see a written indicative term sheet from each lender, not a verbal summary.
- Please tell us who pays you and how much, including any fee from lenders.

**Please also tell us**
- Which lenders you can access, and whether you are regulated for this kind of lending (your registration number).
- Whether a scheme like ours is likely to be fundable at all, and what would make it stronger.

We are talking to more than one broker. We will reply to all by [date].
