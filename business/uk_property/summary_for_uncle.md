# One-page summary for my uncle (30 Oct 2026)

I am asking whether you would be the UK-resident director of a new company for one small property project. You would sign documents, meet the bank and solicitors, and visit the site now and then. I would find the deals, run the numbers and do the paperwork from India.

**Nothing is bought and nothing is signed.** No site has passed my own rules yet.

**What you could lose:** any cash you choose to put in. If you sign a bank guarantee, possibly more. I will not ask you to sign an unlimited guarantee. You would use your own solicitor.

**What you could gain:** a share of the profit, agreed in writing before we start. It is not guaranteed. My example deal lost money in most test runs, so we only go ahead with a deal that clears strict rules.

**The rules:** planned profit of at least 20% of the sale value; still break-even if the sale price is 15% lower; planning permission before we buy; a fixed-price builder with 10% contingency; no uncapped personal guarantee.

**Where things stand:** I checked nine public plot listings. The two that fit the budget did not work. A single small home in outer London is hard to make profitable at today's prices. Next I will look for a better site, get written loan terms from a broker, and compare three development managers.

**Three questions:** (1) Are you open to this in principle? (2) How much time could you give? (3) What is the one thing you would refuse to sign?

You can say no, or stop, at any point before you sign.

Full detail, with the numbers and the stress test, is on the family page.
