# SME IPO merchant bankers (lead managers): shortlist data

File: `sme_merchant_bankers.csv` (107 rows; the source page said 109, two cards did not parse).
Columns: name, city, ipos_so_far, funds_raised_cr, avg_ipo_size_cr, avg_listing_gain_pct, profile_url.
Sorted by number of SME IPOs managed.

## Source and date
- ipoplatform.com "List of SME Merchant Bankers" (https://www.ipoplatform.com/merchant-bankers), read 2026-10-19.
- That site is a commercial IPO-consulting firm and a lead-generation business. Its figures are NOT verified against exchange or SEBI records. Treat them as a starting list, not as facts.

## What to check before you trust a name (do these yourself, they are free)
1. Registration: the banker must be a SEBI-registered Merchant Banker (Category I). Search the SEBI "Intermediaries / Merchant Bankers" register at sebi.gov.in and check the registration is valid and not suspended.
2. Orders or actions: search SEBI orders for the firm's name (penalties, bans).
3. Track record on the exchange you want: NSE Emerge or BSE SME. The exchanges publish each issue's lead manager in the prospectus (offer document).
4. After-listing behaviour: price after 6 to 12 months, not just listing day gain. A high average listing gain says little about whether the stock held its price.
5. Market maker: SME IPOs need a market maker for at least 3 years (check current rules). Ask who it is and the fee.

Official starting points (names only; verify the links yourself): NSE Emerge "Selecting a Merchant Banker" and "Participants" pages; BSE SME; SEBI's register of merchant bankers.

## Not covered
Fees, retainer terms, eligibility rules and timelines are not in this file. Ask each shortlisted banker for a written fee note and compare at least three.
