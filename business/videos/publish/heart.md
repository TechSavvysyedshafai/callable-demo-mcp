## Description
Why does your heart go lub-dub? One heartbeat is one cardiac cycle, and at about 72 beats a minute it lasts 0.8 seconds.
Atria contract, ventricles contract and the AV valves slam shut (LUB), the semilunar valves open to the pulmonary artery and aorta, then everything relaxes and the semilunar valves close (DUB).
