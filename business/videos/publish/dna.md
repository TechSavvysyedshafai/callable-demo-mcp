## Description
How does one DNA become two identical copies? E. coli copies about 2,000 base pairs every second.
Helicase unzips the strands at the origin, each old strand is a template, DNA polymerase builds 5′ → 3′ (leading strand smoothly, lagging strand in Okazaki fragments), and DNA ligase joins the pieces. Each new molecule keeps one old strand: semi-conservative.
