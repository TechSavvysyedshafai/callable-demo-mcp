## Description
One cell becomes two identical cells. How? Four phases: PMAT.
Prophase (chromatin condenses, nuclear envelope disappears), metaphase (chromosomes line up at the equator on spindle fibres), anaphase (centromeres split, chromatids pulled apart), telophase (nuclear envelope forms again), then cytokinesis.
