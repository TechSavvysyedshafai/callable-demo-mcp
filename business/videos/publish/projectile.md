## Description
Drop one ball, throw another sideways. Which lands first? Both hit the ground together, because horizontal and vertical motion are independent.
Split the launch speed into vx = u cos θ and vy = u sin θ, then: T = 2u sin θ / g, H = u² sin²θ / 2g, R = u² sin 2θ / g. Range is largest at 45°, and 30° and 60° give the same range. The path is a parabola.
