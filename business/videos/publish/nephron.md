180 litres filtered, 1.5 litres of urine: where does the rest go?

## Description
You make about 180 litres of filtrate a day but only 1 to 1.5 litres of urine. Where does the rest go?
Three steps in the nephron: filtration in the glomerulus into Bowman's capsule, reabsorption in the PCT and loop of Henle, secretion in the DCT, then the collecting duct takes back more water.
