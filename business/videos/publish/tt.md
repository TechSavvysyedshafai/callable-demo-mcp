## Description
How does DNA make a protein? Two steps: transcription, then translation.
RNA polymerase opens the DNA at the promoter and builds mRNA from the template strand (U replaces T). The mRNA leaves through a nuclear pore, and the ribosome reads it three bases at a time from the start codon AUG; tRNA brings each amino acid, the chain grows by peptide bonds and is released at a stop codon.
