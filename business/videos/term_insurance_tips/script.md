# Video 1: 3 things to check before buying term insurance (India)

Format: vertical 9:16, about 35 seconds, AI voice (disclosed), text on screen.

## Narration (about 85 words)
Buying term insurance in India? Check three things first.

One: cover. A common rule of thumb is ten to fifteen times your yearly income, minus your loans. It is a starting point, not a rule.

Two: tell the truth on the form. Smoking, health, other policies. A wrong answer can get a claim rejected later.

Three: look at the claim record. Insurers publish their claim settlement ratio. Compare a few years, not just one number.

This is general information, not advice. Ask a licensed adviser before you buy.
