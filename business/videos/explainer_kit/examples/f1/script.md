## Facts
- Downforce and drag rise with the square of speed (force = 1/2 x air density x speed squared x area x coefficient). Textbook aerodynamics.
- DRS opens a flap in the rear wing to cut drag on straights; it also cuts rear downforce. Used in F1 2011-2025.
  2026 rules replaced DRS with active aerodynamics on front and rear wings (verify against the FIA 2026 technical regulations before publishing).
- Lower ride height speeds up the air under the floor (ground effect) and adds suction, until the floor gets too close and stalls. The video's ride-height chart shows this.
