# Show notes: Episode 1

**Building is easy now. Deciding what to build is the job.**

We read Anthropic's 36-page "Founder's Playbook: Building an AI-Native Startup" (May 2026) and pull out, stage by stage, the goal, the trap and one thing to do this week.

Chapters
- 0:00 The 42% problem
- 1:00 What changed: money, headcount and skill stop being the blockers
- 2:30 Idea: a testable problem, and "confirmation bias now comes with a research engine"
- 5:00 MVP: CLAUDE.md as project memory, scope documents, success numbers set before launch
- 7:30 Launch: when the founder becomes the bottleneck
- 9:00 Scale: would your users stay if someone copied you?
- 10:00 Three things to do this week

Three things to do this week
1. Write your problem as one testable sentence: who, how often, how bad, what they do today.
2. Ask your AI tool for the three strongest reasons your idea is wrong.
3. Write your day-7 and day-30 success numbers before you launch.

Source: Anthropic, The Founder's Playbook (download it from Anthropic or claude.com). Not affiliated with Anthropic.
