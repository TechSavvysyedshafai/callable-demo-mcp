# Google Business Profile kit (copy, paste, fill the [brackets])

Free. Do it from your phone: open the Google Maps app, search your shop, tap "Claim this business" or "Add your business". Google will ask you to verify (video, phone, or postcard). Only you can do that step.

## Rules that keep your profile safe (Google can suspend it otherwise)
- Business name = your real shop name only. Do not add words like "best jeans shop Patna" to the name.
- Use only true statements. If you write "swap within 7 days", you must really do it.
- Do not buy or write fake reviews. Ask real customers.
- Use a real address, a phone that is answered, and real opening hours.

## 1. Basic facts (fill in)
- Shop name: [name exactly as on your signboard]
- Address: [shop no., road, area, Patna, pincode]
- Phone: [number that is answered]
- WhatsApp: [number]
- Hours: [e.g. 10:30 am to 9:00 pm, closed on ...]
- Opened: [year] (25 years)

## 2. Category
- Main: Clothing store
- Extra (pick those that fit): Jeans store, Men's clothing store, T-shirt store, Clothing alteration service (only if you really offer alterations)

## 3. Description (English, under 750 characters)
[Shop name] has been a trusted store in Patna for jeans and T-shirts for 25 years. Try anything on, and swap it within 7 days with your bill. We measure our sizes so the tag matches the fit. Slim, straight, relaxed and baggy cuts [and large sizes up to ___]. Students: ask about our student offer. Visit us at [area], Patna.

[दुकान का नाम] पिछले 25 सालों से पटना में जींस और टी-शर्ट के लिए भरोसेमंद नाम है। कोई भी कपड़ा पहनकर देखें और बिल के साथ 7 दिन में बदलें। हम साइज़ नापकर बताते हैं, ताकि टैग और फिटिंग एक जैसी हो। स्लिम, स्ट्रेट, रिलैक्स्ड और बैगी फिट उपलब्ध [और बड़े साइज़ ___ तक]। छात्रों के लिए खास ऑफर के बारे में पूछें। पता: [इलाका], पटना।

## 4. Products to list (add a photo and a price range for each)

- Large sizes: [waist 40 and above], if you stock them
- Services: try before you buy, 7-day exchange, free hemming/length alteration [only if true]

## 5. Photos (take with your phone, in daylight)
1. Shop front with signboard (clear, straight on)
2. Inside, wide shot of the shelves
3. A staff member helping a customer (get permission)
4. The trial room (clean)
5. 5 to 8 best-selling jeans, folded and flat on a table
6. 5 to 8 T-shirts, flat on a table
7. The "Try it on, swap in 7 days" sign
Add 2 new photos every week.

## 6. Questions and answers to add (you can post them yourself and answer them)
- Q: Can I swap if the size is wrong? A: Yes, within 7 days with the bill. [only if true]
- Q: Do you have large sizes? A: [Yes, up to waist __ / No]
- Q: Do you give a student discount? A: [Yes, __% with a student ID / No]
- Q: Is there parking nearby? A: [answer]

## 7. Weekly posts (Updates tab, 5 minutes each)
- Week 1: new stock photo + "Try before you buy, swap within 7 days."
- Week 2: "Student offer: [offer]. Show your ID."
- Week 3: festival or wedding stock photo (Chhath, Diwali, Eid, wedding season)
- Week 4: "Tell us what you could not find. We will try to get it." (This feeds your lost-sale log.)

## 8. Asking for reviews (send to customers who said they were happy)
English: Thank you for shopping at [shop name]! If you liked it, please leave us a short review: [your review link]. It helps others find us.
Hindi: [दुकान का नाम] से खरीदारी के लिए धन्यवाद! अगर आपको अच्छा लगा तो यहाँ छोटा सा रिव्यू दें: [रिव्यू लिंक]। इससे दूसरे लोगों को हमारी दुकान ढूँढने में मदद मिलेगी।
(Your review link: open your profile, tap "Ask for reviews", copy the link.)
Reply to every review within 2 days. Thank the happy ones. For an unhappy one, say sorry and offer to fix it in the shop.

## 9. WhatsApp message to old customers
[Shop name], Patna: new stock of jeans and T-shirts is in. Try anything, swap within 7 days with your bill. Students: ask for the student offer. Please forward this to a friend. Location: [Google Maps link]
Hindi: [दुकान का नाम], पटना: जींस और टी-शर्ट का नया स्टॉक आ गया है। कुछ भी पहनकर देखें, बिल के साथ 7 दिन में बदलें। छात्रों के लिए खास ऑफर। अपने दोस्तों को भी भेजें। लोकेशन: [गूगल मैप्स लिंक]
Send only to people who have bought from you. Do not send many messages. About one a week at most.

## 10. How to know it is working
- Every customer at the counter: "How did you hear about us?" Tally: Google / WhatsApp / Instagram / friend / passing by / other.
- In your profile, once a month, write down: calls, "get directions" taps, and profile views.
- Keep what works. Drop what does not after 4 weeks.
