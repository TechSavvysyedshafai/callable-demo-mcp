Fill every [BRACKET] with your real detail before pasting. Promise only what is true (swap days, discounts, hours).
Where to paste: WhatsApp Business app > Settings (or Tools) > Business tools. Each item below names the screen.

## 2. Greeting message (Business tools > Greeting message)
Sent once when a new customer writes, or after 14 days of silence.

EN: Hello! Welcome to [shop name]. Ask us about jeans and T-shirts: send the size, colour or budget and we will reply soon. Open [hours]. Address: [address]

HI: धन्यवाद! अभी दुकान बंद है। हम [समय] पर खुलेंगे और आपको जवाब देंगे। आप साइज़ और पसंद लिखकर छोड़ दें।

## 5. Catalogue captions (Business tools > Catalogue; one item = photo, name, price, description)
Pattern (copy per product, use real price and sizes):
- Name: [Slim fit jeans / Regular fit jeans / Round-neck T-shirt / Polo T-shirt]
- Price: [₹ real price]
- Description HI: [रंग], [फिट]। साइज़: [28-38]। पहनकर देखने के लिए दुकान आएं।
- Description EN: [colour], [fit]. Sizes: [28-38]. Come try it on at the shop.
Start with your 10 best sellers. Photo tips: plain wall, daylight, one clear front shot, one close-up of fabric or stitching.

## 6. Labels (Chats > long-press > Label)
New customer, Asked price, Wants size/stock, Bought, Gave consent (YES), Opted out (STOP).
Sort the "Opted out" label out of every broadcast list the same day.

## 7. Rules for broadcasts
- Message only people who said YES to /optin (keep their reply as proof, with the date).
- At most one broadcast a week per customer. Do not message people between 8 pm and 9 am.
- The app's broadcast list only reaches people who saved your number.
- If someone writes STOP, reply once to confirm and never message again.
- Do not buy or scrape phone-number lists.
