# Outreach drafts for the Deal Screen test

Rules for every message:
- Use only public contact details or places where the community allows offers. No LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram or Reddit scraping.
- Every email gets an opt-out line and a real postal address (CAN-SPAM). Keep a "do not contact" list and honour it.
- One follow-up at most. No sequences.
- Say it is a test and free for the first five.

## A. Community post (Searchfunder, Skool "Searcher School" or similar; read each group's rules first)
Subject: Free pre-offer screen for 5 buyers, looking for feedback

I built a short written screen for small-business listings: the seller's numbers labelled copied / derived / assumed, a price check, red flags and ten questions to put to the seller. It is not a quality-of-earnings report and no CPA signs it.

I am looking for 5 buyers with a real listing in mind (about $100K to $2M) who will let me run it free in return for honest feedback. Reply here or message me and I will send a sample.

## Feedback questions after each free screen
1. What did you do with it (bid, pass, ask the seller)?
2. What was missing?
3. What would you have paid for this, in dollars?
4. Would you use it again on the next listing?
