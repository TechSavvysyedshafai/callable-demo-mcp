# Pre-offer Deal Screen (one page)

Status: draft for testing. Prices and turnaround are assumptions to test, not facts.

## What it is
A 3-page written screen of one small-business listing, done before you spend money on a CPA. You send the listing and the seller's profit-and-loss figures. You get back:

1. A summary and a plain go / no-go / need-more-information call.
2. The seller's numbers with each figure labelled copied, derived or assumed.
3. Price check: what the stated multiple implies, and a range to bid.
4. Red flags (concentration, falling trend, owner dependence, add-backs that look soft).
5. Ten questions to put to the seller.

## Who it is for
Buyers of businesses roughly $100K to $2M who are looking at their first or second deal and want a second pair of eyes before an offer.

## What it is not
- Not a quality-of-earnings report. No CPA signs it.
- Not legal, tax or investment advice.
- It cannot verify figures against bank statements unless you send them.
- It will say what is missing instead of guessing.

## How it works
- You send: listing link or CIM, 2 to 3 years of profit-and-loss figures, anything else you have.
- You get the screen within [48 hours, to be tested].
- Confidential material: I will sign your NDA. Files are deleted [30 days] after delivery. Send only what the screen needs.

## Price (to test)
- First 5 buyers: free, in exchange for honest feedback and, if you agree, a short quote.
- After that: [$99] per screen. Ask each tester: "Would you have paid this?"
